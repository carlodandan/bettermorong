# Sangguniang Bayan Resolutions — Morong, Rizal

The **Sangguniang Bayan ng Morong** regularly passes municipal resolutions expressing the official position of the local legislative body, approving contracts and agreements entered into by the Municipal Mayor, accrediting civil society organizations, and adopting local development plans.

---

## Types of Council Resolutions

1. **Adoption of Municipal Plans**:
   - Resolutions approving the Comprehensive Development Plan (CDP), Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP), Local Disaster Risk Reduction Management Plan (LDRRMP), and Annual Investment Program (AIP).
2. **Accreditation of Civil Society Organizations (CSOs)**:
   - Official accreditation of NGOs, cooperatives, transport associations, and people's organizations to participate in local special bodies (e.g., Municipal Development Council, Local Health Board, Local School Board).
3. **Authorization of Bilateral Agreements**:
   - Resolutions authorizing the Municipal Mayor to sign Memoranda of Agreement (MOA) with national government agencies (DOH, DA, DILG, DSWD, DOST) and external partners.
4. **Commendations and In Memoriam**:
   - Formal recognition of outstanding Morongueño athletes, scholars, cultural contributors, and public servants.

---

## How to Apply for CSO Accreditation

Civil society organizations, farmer/fisherfolk groups, and community associations seeking SB accreditation must submit the following to the Office of the Sangguniang Bayan Secretary:

1. Formal letter of application addressed to the Vice Mayor and SB Council.
2. Duly accomplished CSO Application Form.
3. Certificate of Registration from SEC, CDA, or DOLE.
4. Constitution and By-Laws.
5. List of current officers and members with addresses.
6. Financial statements of the preceding calendar year.
7. Profile of programs and community activities conducted in Morong.
