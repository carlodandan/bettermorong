# Citizen’s Charter 2025 — Municipality of Morong, Rizal

In full compliance with **Republic Act No. 9485** (Anti-Red Tape Act of 2007) and **Republic Act No. 11032** (The Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018), the **Official Citizen’s Charter (2025 Edition)** of the Municipality of Morong establishes public service standards, statutory fees, checklists of requirements, and processing windows across all 18 frontline municipal departments.

---

## 🏛️ Citizen's Charter Guarantees & Service Standards

Under national anti-red tape mandates, every resident, business owner, and visitor transacting with the Municipal Government of Morong is entitled to:

1. **Zero Hidden Fees**: No regulatory fee, charge, or assessment may be collected without an enacted municipal ordinance and an official municipal treasury receipt.
2. **Statutory Processing Turnaround Windows**:
   - **Simple Transactions**: Maximum of **3 working days** (most Morong frontline services process within 5 to 60 minutes).
   - **Complex Transactions**: Maximum of **7 working days** (requires evaluation or multi-stage verification).
   - **Highly Technical Applications**: Maximum of **20 working days** (requires technical committee or Sanggunian deliberation).
3. **Strict No-Noon-Break Policy**: Frontline payment windows, civil registry, health triage, and permit intake are manned continuously from **8:00 AM to 5:00 PM**, Monday through Friday.
4. **No Unnecessary Requirements**: Offices cannot require documents already available in municipal records.
5. **Anti-Red Tape Unit & PACD**: The Public Assistance and Complaints Desk (PACD) is stationed at the Ground Floor Lobby of the Municipal Hall with an Officer of the Day.

## 📢 Public Redress, ARTA & Complaints Mechanism

If you experience undue processing delays, discourtesy, fixers, or unlawful fee demands:

1. **Morong Public Assistance & Complaints Desk (PACD)**:
   - Approach the Officer of the Day at the Ground Floor Lobby, New Municipal Building, Sitio Paglabas, Brgy. San Pedro.
   - Text or call the Morong Civic Assistance line: **0956 099 2259** / **(02) 7212-5741**.
2. **Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA)**:
   - Hotline: **8-478-5093** | Email: `complaints@arta.gov.ph`
3. **Presidential Complaint Center (PCC)**:
   - Dial **8888** from any mobile or landline nationwide.
4. **Contact Center ng Bayan (CCB)**:
   - SMS: **0908 881 6565** | Hotline: **1-6565**
