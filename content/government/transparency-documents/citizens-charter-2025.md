# Citizen’s Charter 2025 — Municipality of Morong, Rizal

In full compliance with **Republic Act No. 11032** (The Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018), the **Citizen’s Charter 2025** of the Municipality of Morong sets official service standards, timelines, fees, and procedures across all local frontline offices.

---

## What the Citizen’s Charter Guarantees

Under RA 11032, every citizen transacting with the municipal government is entitled to:

1. **Zero Hidden Fees**: No fee may be collected without an official municipal ordinance and an official government receipt.
2. **Fixed Processing Times**:
   - **Simple Transactions**: Maximum of **3 working days**
   - **Complex Transactions**: Maximum of **7 working days**
   - **Highly Technical Applications**: Maximum of **20 working days**
3. **No-Noon-Break Policy**: Continuous service during regular working hours (8:00 AM to 5:00 PM), with frontline windows manned through the midday break.
4. **Anti-Red Tape Unit & Feedback**: Every transaction includes an official client satisfaction measurement and feedback mechanism.

## Frontline Services Covered in the Charter

| Office                               | Core Frontline Services Covered                                                | Standard Processing Window |
| :----------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------- |
| **Municipal Mayor’s Office**         | Mayor’s Clearance, Endorsement Letters, AICS financial approvals               | 1 – 3 days                 |
| **Local Civil Registry Office**      | Birth, Marriage, Death Registration, CTC of vital records, RA 9048 corrections | Same day to 3 days         |
| **Municipal Health Office**          | Outpatient consultations, immunization, prenatal care, sanitary permits        | Immediate to 1 day         |
| **Municipal Social Welfare (MSWDO)** | Senior Citizen ID, PWD ID, Solo Parent ID, crisis intake                       | 1 – 3 days                 |
| **Municipal Treasurer’s Office**     | Real Property Tax (amilyar), Business Tax payments, Cedula                     | Immediate (10 – 30 mins)   |
| **Municipal Assessor’s Office**      | Tax Declarations, Property verifications, certified records                    | 1 – 3 days                 |
| **Municipal Engineering Office**     | Building, Electrical, Fencing, Demolition, Occupancy Permits                   | 3 – 7 days                 |
| **Planning & Development (MPDO)**    | Locational / Zoning Clearances                                                 | 1 – 3 days                 |
| **MENRO**                            | Environmental Clearances, Solid Waste collection compliance                    | 1 – 2 days                 |
| **Municipal Agriculture Office**     | RSBSA registration, boat & fisherfolk licensing, seed assistance               | 1 – 2 days                 |

## How to File Feedback or Complaints

If you encounter unreasonable delays, fixers, or discourteous staff:

- **Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA) Hotline**: Dial **8-478-5093** / Email `complaints@arta.gov.ph`
- **Contact Center ng Bayan (CCB)**: SMS **0908-881-6565** / Call **1-6565**
- **Morong Public Assistance & Complaints Desk (PACD)**: Located at the Ground Floor Lobby, New Municipal Building, Sitio Paglabas, San Pedro, Morong, Rizal.
