# Assistance to Municipalities (AM) — Morong, Rizal

The **Assistance to Municipalities (AM)** program is an infrastructure support initiative overseen by the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) to finance priority municipal projects focusing on disaster resilience, flood control, and potable water access.

---

## Funded Priority Projects in Morong

The Municipality of Morong has implemented critical community infrastructure projects supported through the Assistance to Municipalities grant program:

## Project Monitoring & Citizen Feedback

- **Implementing Office**: Office of the Municipal Engineer in coordination with the Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO).
- **Public Inspections**: Citizens and civil society organizations may inspect public worksites and review posted billboard notices showing project scope, contractor name, timeline, and allocated budget.
- **Reporting Concerns**: To report issues regarding municipal public works, contact the Engineering Office at `engineering.morongrizal@gmail.com` or call `(02) 7212-5741`.
