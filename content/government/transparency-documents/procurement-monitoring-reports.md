# Procurement Monitoring Reports (PMR) & Bids Repository — Morong, Rizal

In compliance with Republic Act No. 9184 (Government Procurement Reform Act) and Government Procurement Policy Board (GPPB) regulations, the **Bids and Awards Committee (BAC)** of the Municipality of Morong publishes its procurement monitoring records, bid notices, and contract awards for public transparency.

---

## What is the Bids and Awards Committee (BAC)?

The Bids and Awards Committee (BAC) is the official municipal body established under Republic Act No. 9184 responsible for ensuring that all government procurement—ranging from infrastructure and road concreting to medical supplies and municipal equipment—is conducted through open, competitive, and transparent processes.

The BAC is assisted by:

- **BAC Secretariat**: Serves as the administrative support unit handling document distribution, minutes, and public bids archives.
- **Technical Working Group (TWG)**: Evaluates technical specifications, eligibility requirements, and post-qualification compliance of bidders.
- **Official Observers**: Representatives from the Commission on Audit (COA) and accredited civil society organizations (CSOs) invited to oversee bidding proceedings.

---

## Key Procurement Concepts Explained for Citizens

Understanding government procurement terms helps residents track how local public funds are utilized:

- **Approved Budget for the Contract (ABC)**:  
  The maximum budget approved by the Sanggunian / Head of the Procuring Entity for a specific project. By law, no bid can exceed the ABC; bids higher than the ABC are automatically rejected during opening.
- **Invitation to Bid (ITB)**:  
  The official public notice announcing an upcoming procurement opportunity, outlining eligibility requirements, project scope, submission deadlines, and bid opening schedules.
- **Notice of Award (NOA)**:  
  The formal letter issued to the winning bidder whose offer was evaluated as the _Lowest Calculated and Responsive Bid (LCRB)_ for goods/infrastructure, confirming the award of the municipal contract.
- **Notice to Proceed (NTP)**:  
  The written directive instructing the awarded supplier or contractor to begin project execution within a specified start date and calendar duration.
- **Procurement Monitoring Report (PMR)**:  
  A semi-annual comprehensive report submitted to the Government Procurement Policy Board (GPPB) summarizing all procurement projects, methods used, contract values, variances, and completion milestones.

## How Residents Can Access Procurement Records (FOI & Citizen's Charter)

Under the Philippine Constitution (Art. III, Sec. 7) and Executive Order No. 2, s. 2016 (Freedom of Information), citizens, civil society groups, and media organizations have the right to inspect public procurement documents:

1. **Identify the Document**: Note the project name, location (e.g., barangay road concreting), and approximate bidding year or PhilGEPS reference number.
2. **Submit a Written Request**: File a formal letter of inquiry or standard FOI request form addressed to the **BAC Secretariat Office** (Ground Floor, Morong Municipal Building, Sitio Paglabas, Brgy. San Pedro).
3. **Present Valid Identification**: Bring any government-issued ID (such as PhilSys National ID, Voter's ID, or Driver's License).
4. **Processing Timeline**: In accordance with the Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA) standards and Republic Act No. 6713, requests for public records are processed within fifteen (15) working days, provided the document does not contain proprietary trade secrets or ongoing litigation exceptions.
5. **COA Citizen Auditing**: Residents observing unfinished projects, substandard materials, or abandonment may also submit verifiable reports directly to the **Commission on Audit (COA) Resident Auditor** at Morong Municipal Hall or via the COA Citizens' Desk.
