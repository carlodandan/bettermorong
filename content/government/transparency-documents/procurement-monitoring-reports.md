# Procurement Monitoring Reports (PMR) — Morong, Rizal

In compliance with Republic Act No. 9184 (Government Procurement Reform Act) and Government Procurement Policy Board (GPPB) guidelines, the **Bids and Awards Committee (BAC)** of the Municipality of Morong publishes its Procurement Monitoring Reports (PMR).

---

## What is a Procurement Monitoring Report?

The Procurement Monitoring Report (PMR) covers all procurement activities undertaken by the procuring entity from the pre-procurement conference up to the award and acceptance of contracts. It details:

- Project identification and description
- Approved Budget for the Contract (ABC)
- Method of procurement (Public Bidding, Alternative Methods)
- Actual procurement timelines and status
- Contract cost and winning supplier or contractor
- Variance analysis and delivery milestones
