# Downloadable Application Forms & Citizen Guidelines — Morong, Rizal

In compliance with **Republic Act No. 11032** (The Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018), the Municipality of Morong provides downloadable application forms, engineering templates, ARTA unified business sheets, and Citizen’s Charter handbooks for public use.

---

## Ease of Doing Business Guarantees (RA 11032)

Every citizen and business owner filing an application with the municipal government is guaranteed:

1. **Clear Documentary Checklists**: Frontline offices must provide a complete checklist of requirements before accepting an application. No additional requirements may be demanded after submission.
2. **Fixed Processing Timelines**:
   - **Simple Transactions**: Maximum of **three (3) working days** (e.g., Cedula, Health Card, Working Permit).
   - **Complex Transactions**: Maximum of **seven (7) working days** (e.g., Building Permits, Business Permits).
   - **Highly Technical Applications**: Maximum of **twenty (20) working days** (e.g., Specialized engineering or multi-agency zoning clearances).
3. **Zero Hidden Fees**: All assessment fees, regulatory charges, and clearances must be backed by an enacted municipal tax ordinance and issued an official government receipt.
4. **No-Noon-Break Policy**: Frontline receiving desks at the New Municipal Building remain staffed and operational continuously from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM on official working days.

## Frontline Departments & Application Categories

- **Office of the Municipal Engineer / Building Official (OBO)**:  
  Processes applications for Building Permits, Electrical Permits, Demolition, Excavation, Fencing, and Certificates of Occupancy in accordance with the National Building Code of the Philippines (PD 1096).
- **Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)**:  
  Administers the Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS), unified ARTA application forms, annual renewals, and municipal business tax assessments.
- **Municipal Health Office (MHO)**:  
  Conducts medical laboratory screening and issues Sanitary Permits for food establishments, commercial stalls, and rental properties.
- **Office of the Municipal Mayor & Treasury**:  
  Issues Mayor’s Occupational Working Permits, Community Tax Certificates (Cedula), and official payment receipts.
- **Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO)**:  
  Evaluates zoning conformance and issues Locational Clearances.
