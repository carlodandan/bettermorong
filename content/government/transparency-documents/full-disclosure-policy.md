# Full Disclosure Policy & Annual Investment Plans — Morong, Rizal

The **Full Disclosure Policy (FDP)** is a national mandate established by the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) requiring local government units to fully disclose their financial transactions, municipal budgets, and expenditures to promote public trust and integrity.

---

## What Documents Are Disclosed?

The Municipality of Morong regularly posts and uploads the following mandatory financial disclosures:

1. **Annual Budget Report**: Summary of municipal executive appropriations and revenue targets approved by the Sangguniang Bayan.
2. **Annual Investment Program (AIP)**: Detailed capital expenditure program for social services, economic enterprises, infrastructure, and disaster resilience.
3. **Statement of Receipts and Expenditures (SRE)**: Quarterly and annual accounting of actual revenues collected versus expenditures incurred.
4. **Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Fund (LDRRMF)**: Utilization report on the 5% disaster fund allocated for mitigation and relief operations.
5. **Special Education Fund (SEF) Utilization**: Allocation and disbursement of the 1% additional real property tax earmarked for public schools and classrooms.
6. **20% Development Fund Utilization**: Report on the 20% component of the National Tax Allotment (NTA) dedicated to socio-economic development projects.
7. **Local Government Support Fund (LGSF-FALGU & LGSF-DRRAP)**: Disclosures on national grant allocations for local roads, disaster rehabilitation, and municipal facilities.

## Access the DILG Full Disclosure Portal

Morong's official submitted financial reports can be verified directly on the national DILG portal:

- **DILG FDP Portal**: [https://fdpp.dilg.gov.ph/](https://fdpp.dilg.gov.ph/)
- **Physical Posting**: Municipal Transparency Seal Billboard located at the Ground Floor Lobby, Morong Municipal Hall, Sitio Paglabas, San Pedro, Morong, Rizal.
