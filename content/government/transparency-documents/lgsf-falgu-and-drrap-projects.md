# LGSF-FALGU, DRRAP & Special Development Disclosures — Morong, Rizal

The **Local Government Support Fund (LGSF)** comprises specialized national grant facilities administered by the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) and the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) to assist local government units with critical infrastructure, disaster recovery, public markets, and social protection programs.

---

## 1. LGSF-FALGU (Financial Assistance to Local Government Units)

The Municipality of Morong has received and implemented funding under the national **LGSF-FALGU** program for targeted economic recovery and municipal enterprise upgrades:

### Construction of Public Market Vegetable and Fruit Section

- **Allocation Amount**: **PhP 9,000,000.00**
- **Funding Agency**: Department of Budget and Management (DBM)
- **Project Purpose**: Modernization of the municipal wet and dry market facility in Sitio Paglabas, Barangay San Pedro. Construction of clean, dedicated trading sections for local agricultural produce, vegetable vendors, and fruit merchants.
- **Implementation Status**: Completed and in active public operation.
- **Public Disclosures**:
  - Notice of Award & Notice to Proceed (DBM-approved)
  - Quarterly Report on Fund Utilization and Project Status (uploaded as of December 2022)

### Municipal Emergency Response: Advance Life Support (ALS) Ambulance

- **Procurement**: Acquisition of 1 Unit of specialized **Advance Life Support (ALS) Ambulance** equipped with cardiac monitors, oxygen delivery systems, and emergency trauma equipment.
- **Deployment**: Assigned to the Morong Municipal Health Office (MHO) and MDRRMO Operations Center for emergency hospital transfers to Rizal Provincial Hospital System (RPHS Morong Main) and specialized tertiary medical centers.
- **Public Disclosures**: Fund utilization reports for Q1 and Q2 2023.

## 2. LGSF-DRRAP (Disaster Rehabilitation and Reconstruction Assistance Program)

Following devastating typhoons in Region IV-A (including Typhoon Ulysses), the Municipality of Morong utilized **LGSF-DRRAP** funds for emergency recovery and rebuilding:

### Shelter Construction Materials Distribution

- **Beneficiaries**: Families and residents in low-lying lakeside and riverbank communities whose homes sustained partial or total structural damage.
- **Package Components**: Distribution of roofing corrugated sheets, timber framing, cement, and basic construction materials.
- **Reporting & Auditing**:
  - LGSF-DRRAP Report on Fund Utilization and Status of Program - Project Implementation (June 2021)
  - LGSF-DRRAP Report on Fund Utilization and Status of Program - Project Implementation (September 2021)
  - Full beneficiary manifests certified by the Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO) and Municipal Engineering Office.

## 3. LCAT-VAWC (Anti-Trafficking & Violence Against Women and Children)

Under DILG Memorandum Circulars and Republic Act No. 9262 (Anti-Violence Against Women and Their Children Act of 2004), Morong maintains an active **Local Committee on Anti-Trafficking and Violence Against Women and their Children (LCAT-VAWC)**:

### Annual Reports & Narrative Disclosures

- **2023 Annual Report (AR)**: Accomplishment report on barangay VAW desk functionality, case intake, and inter-agency referrals.
- **2025 Narrative Report**: Multi-year strategic narrative submitted to the DILG evaluating community anti-trafficking checkpoints, educational advocacy in public schools, and livelihood assistance for vulnerable women and youth.

### Emergency Assistance & Protection Desks

If you or someone you know is experiencing domestic violence, abuse, or human trafficking, reach out immediately:

- **MSWDO Frontline Assistance**: Ground Floor, Morong Municipal Hall (`mswd.22@gmail.com`)
- **PNP Morong Women & Children Protection Desk (WCPD)**: `0906-513-0718` / `0998-598-5725`

- **National 24/7 Hotline**: **911** or DSWD Hotline **1343** (Anti-Trafficking)
