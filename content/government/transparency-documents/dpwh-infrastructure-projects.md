### FY 2026 Detailed Pipeline Profiles

#### 1. Morong Bypass Road Extension — Brgy. CCL (Contract ID: `26DO0104`)

- **Program Year**: **2026 Infrastructure Program**
- **Project Scope**: Organizational Outcome 1: Ensure Safe and Reliable National Road System — Network Development Program — Construction of By-pass and Diversion Roads. Next-phase extension of the Morong Bypass Road (Sta. 2+144.55 to Sta. 2+878.55) traversing Barangay CCL (Calero-Can-Iba-Lagundi), opening direct economic access to agricultural and residential outskirts.
- **Category**: Roads: Construction - Gravel (Bypass Corridor)
- **Current Status**: **For Procurement / Pre-Bidding**
- **Responsible Office**: DPWH Rizal 2nd District Engineering Office (Rizal 2nd DEO)
- **Region**: Region IV-A (CALABARZON)

#### 2. Binangonan–Morong Boundary Connecting Bridge (Contract ID: `26DN0101`)

- **Program Year**: **2026 Infrastructure Program**
- **Project Scope**: Construction of a new concrete superstructure bridge with cast-in-place piles connecting Binangonan and Morong (Sta. 1+480 to Sta. 1+500, Binangonan side crossing into Morong First District), creating an alternate Laguna de Bay transit corridor and disaster alternate route.
- **Category**: Bridges: Construction - Concrete (Superstructure) with Cast-in-Place Piles
- **Current Status**: **For Procurement / Pre-Bidding**
- **Responsible Office**: DPWH Rizal 1st District Engineering Office (Rizal 1st DEO)
- **Region**: Region IV-A (CALABARZON)

### FY 2025 Detailed Project Profiles

#### 1. Morong Bypass Road Construction (Contract ID: `25DO0012`)

- **Project Scope**: Network Development Program (Organizational Outcome 1) — Construction of brand-new diversion and bypass lanes designed to decongest the central Morong commercial artery, speed up vehicle turnaround between Teresa, Morong, and Baras, and expand regional logistics access.
- **Budget Allocated**: **₱95,070,857.61**
- **Contractor**: Persan Construction, Inc. (formerly Persan Const.)
- **Contract Start Date**: February 24, 2025
- **Current Physical Progress**: **82.39% Complete**
- **Fund Source**: Regular Infrastructure — GAA 2025 OO-1
- **Coordinates**: `14.5211778° N, 121.2557306° E`
- **Responsible Office**: DPWH Rizal 2nd District Engineering Office (Rizal 2nd DEO)

#### 2. Kay Bulutong CIS Flood Control Structure (Contract ID: `25DO0027`)

- **Project Scope**: Major river management and drainage mitigation works (Organizational Outcome 2) to reinforce embankment walls and safeguard riverside agricultural tracts and vulnerable residential households from seasonal flash flooding.
- **Budget Allocated**: **₱9,763,200.65**
- **Contractor**: GKB Builders
- **Contract Start Date**: February 13, 2025
- **Current Physical Progress**: **80.17% Complete**
- **Fund Source**: Regular Infrastructure — GAA 2025 OO-2
- **Coordinates**: `14.5230556° N, 121.2038417° E`
- **Responsible Office**: DPWH Rizal 2nd District Engineering Office (Rizal 2nd DEO)

#### 3. Cainta-Antipolo-Teresa-Morong Road Pavement Maintenance (Contract ID: `25DN0015`)

- **Project Scope**: Asset Preservation Program — Preventive maintenance asphalt overlay and structural re-blocking across primary highway sections connecting Morong with Antipolo and Teresa (K0020+402 to K0020+594, K0020+800 to K0019+222).
- **Budget Allocated**: **₱14,077,700.00**
- **Contractor**: Readycon Trading & Construction Corp.
- **Project Status**: **100% Completed** (Delivered May 27, 2025)
- **Responsible Office**: DPWH Rizal 1st District Engineering Office (Rizal 1st DEO)

#### 4. Cainta-Antipolo-Teresa-Morong Road Tertiary Section (Contract ID: `25DO0032`)

- **Project Scope**: Asset preservation and safety resurfacing on tertiary connecting road sections (Chainage 000 to Chainage 083) entering the Morong municipal boundary.

#### 6. Rehabilitation of Multi-Purpose Building in Morong (Contract ID: `25DO0104`)

- **Project Scope**: Structural repair, modernization, and roofing reinforcement for municipal community facilities supporting public social services, evacuation shelter readiness, and civic gatherings.
- **Project Status**: **For Procurement / Bidding Stage (FY 2025 GAA)**

#### 7. Intersection Improvement — Corazon C. Aquino Rd & Teresa-Morong Rd (Contract ID: `25DN0179`)

## Citizen Monitoring & Accountability Guidelines

Citizens, barangay officials, civil society organizations, and local business owners are encouraged to actively inspect public infrastructure in their communities:

1. **On-Site Billboard Transparency**: Every DPWH project must display an official billboard at the job site indicating the Project Name, Contractor, Start Date, Target Completion Date, Total Contract Cost, and Project Engineer.
2. **Satellite & Digital Verification**: You can verify coordinates, contracts, and satellite imagery directly on the [DPWH Transparency Portal](https://transparency.dpwh.gov.ph/).
3. **Reporting Substandard Works or Delays**:
   - **DPWH Public Assistance Hotline**: `165-02` (Toll-Free Nationwide)
   - **DPWH Rizal 2nd DEO Contact**: `(02) 8653-5680` / `(02) 8653-5681` (Baras/Morong corridor)
   - **Presidential Citizen Complaint Center**: Call **`8888`** or visit [8888.gov.ph](https://8888.gov.ph)
   - **Civil Society Inquiries**: To lodge municipal coordination questions, contact the Morong Municipal Engineering Office at `(02) 7212-5741`.
