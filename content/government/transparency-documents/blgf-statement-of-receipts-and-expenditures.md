## Key Fiscal Insights for Morong Citizens (FY 2025)

- 🏛️ **NTA Dependency (74.7%)**: The National Tax Allotment (₱264.55M) is Morong's primary operational resource. Local collections reached ₱89.42M (25.3%).
- 🏪 **Business Tax Strength**: Business tax generated **₱38.24M**, proving to be Morong's most robust local income stream.
- 🏥 **Health Prioritization**: Over 54% of social expenditures (**₱23.63M**) directly supported frontline public healthcare and nutrition initiatives.
- 🏗️ **Capital Outlays**: **₱52.59M** was invested into Property, Plant, and Equipment (PPE) to improve public infrastructure and facilities.
