# Demographics & 8 Barangays — BetterMorong

The **Municipality of Morong** is a 2nd-class municipality located in the central part of Rizal Province within Region IV-A (CALABARZON). Strategically nestled between the rolling slopes of the Sierra Madre and the northern shores of Laguna de Bay, Morong serves as a pivotal center of education, historic heritage, and civic commerce.

## Comprehensive Directory of the 8 Component Barangays

Each barangay functions as the primary frontline administrative and service delivery unit of the local government.

### 1. Barangay Bombongan

- **Classification**: Rural / Agricultural & Suburban Residential
- **Official Population**: **4,003 residents** (2024 POPCEN; up from 3,888 in 2020, **+2.96%** growth rate)
- **Contact Number**: `0917-124-5329`
- **Official Email**: `brgybombongan@gmail.com`
- **Key Sitios & Landmarks**: Sitio Dupax, Daang Hari, Bukid Cafe (G. San Antonio St.)
- **Profile**: Bounded by rolling hills and agricultural plains. Home to active rice and vegetable farming clusters alongside scenic agri-tourism and local coffee spots. Connects via Venancio Street to the municipal road network.

### 2. Barangay CCL (Caniogan-Calero-Lanang)

- **Classification**: Urban / Poblacion & Riverside
- **Official Population**: **4,931 residents** (2024 POPCEN; up from 4,917 in 2020, **+0.28%** growth rate)
- **Vision**: A model of a just, clean, progressive, and fully developed barangay through people empowerment and responsive delivery of public services.
- **Key Waterways & Sitios**: Calero Creek, Pantalan Creek, Caniogan, Calero, Lanang
- **Profile**: A historic central riverside barangay situated near the town proper. Farm-to-market trade flows along Calero Creek to Pantalan Creek, providing crucial logistical access for fishermen and smallholder farmers.

### 3. Barangay Maybancal

- **Classification**: Urban / Highway Commercial & Residential
- **Official Population**: **18,224 residents** (2024 POPCEN; up from 17,979 in 2020, **+1.36%** growth rate; accounts for **25.22%** of Morong's total population)
- **Official Address**: Manila East Road, Maybancal, Morong, Rizal 1960
- **Official Email**: `barangaymaybancal@gmail.com`
- **Key Facilities & Flood Control**: Talaga Creek flood mitigation corridor, roadside commerce, daycare centers, and community health outposts.
- **Profile**: Traversed by the major arterial Manila East Road, Maybancal is a bustling residential and commercial gateway with vital connectivity to neighboring towns.

### 4. Barangay Lagundi

- **Classification**: Urban / Mixed Commercial & Tourism
- **Official Population**: **10,126 residents** (2024 POPCEN; up from 10,076 in 2020, **+0.50%** growth rate)
- **Contact Number**: `0917-7105-1636`
- **Key Landmarks**: Manila East Road connection, Laguna de Bay lakeshore access, municipal water facilities, private resorts, and agricultural land.
- **Profile**: Known for its thriving local business establishments, resort tourism, and vital municipal infrastructure including community water supply systems and disaster evacuation facilities.

### 5. Barangay San Guillermo

- **Classification**: Upland / Agricultural & Agri-Tourism
- **Official Population**: **13,089 residents** (2024 POPCEN; up from 12,758 in 2020, **+2.59%** growth rate; added **+331** residents, highest absolute net gain)
- **Key Sitios & Landmarks**: Sitio Labas (Abot Kamay), The Orchard Private Pool Villa, Manila East Lakeview Farms, nature campsites.
- **Profile**: Geographically the largest barangay in land area, San Guillermo features expansive agricultural fields, fruit orchards, and eco-tourism retreats along the scenic foothills overlooking Laguna de Bay.

### 6. Barangay San Jose

- **Classification**: Urban / Poblacion & Agricultural
- **Official Population**: **4,419 residents** (2024 POPCEN; up from 4,293 in 2020, **+2.93%** growth rate)
- **Key Sitios**: Sitio Balante, Sitio Maytigbe, San Jose Proper
- **Facilities**: Barangay Hall with community Health Center, Two (2) Multi-Purpose Covered Courts (Barangay Proper and Upper Balante), and Two (2) Daycare Centers.
- **Profile**: Bounded on the north by Maybancal, south by Bombongan, west by San Guillermo, and separated from San Pedro and San Juan by the Morong River. Predominantly agricultural in upper areas and urbanized along community road networks.

### 7. Barangay San Juan

- **Classification**: Urban / Commercial & Trade Center
- **Official Address**: T. Claudio St., San Juan, Morong, Rizal
- **Official Email**: `brgysanjuanmorong@gmail.com`
- **Official Population**: **11,258 residents** (2024 POPCEN; up from 11,184 in 2020, **+0.66%** growth rate; 15.58% share of Morong total)
- **Land Area**: 478 hectares (Urban Lowland)
- **Economic Profile**: The commercial engine and highest local revenue-remitting barangay of Morong. 60% of residents are employed in business and services, 20% in commercial enterprise, 10% in agriculture, and 10% in fishing.
- **Commercial & Health Landmarks**: SM Savemore, Morong Centerpoint, Jollibee, Mercury Drug, RCBC Savings Bank, D.I.Y Hardware, Rizal Provincial Hospital System (RPHS Morong Main), Morong Doctors Hospital, and Supreme Care Diagnostic Center.

### 8. Barangay San Pedro (Capital Poblacion)

- **Classification**: Urban / Capital Institutional & Education Hub
- **Official Population**: **6,212 residents** (2024 POPCEN; up from 6,056 in 2020, **+2.58%** growth rate)
- **Land Area**: 368.97 hectares
- **Geographic Location**: Bounded on the east by Laguna de Bay, south by Cardona, west by Bombongan, and north by San Jose and San Juan.
- **Seats of Governance**: Host to the Morong Municipal Hall Compound, Sangguniang Bayan Legislative Building, Morong Public Market, and Morong Town Plaza (Morong Park).
- **Center of Education**: Eastern System Colleges of Rizal, Tomas Claudio Memorial College / Renaissance School of Science and Technology, Mapa Prime Academy, St. Claire of Assisi, Timoteo A. Reyes Sr. Memorial Elementary School, and the **Morong, Rizal e-Library** (Gov. Tupas St.).
- **Cultural & Historical Pride**: Seat of the Spanish-era St. Jerome Parish Church facade and ancestral home of renowned Morongueños including **Francisco Feliciano** (2013 National Artist for Music).
