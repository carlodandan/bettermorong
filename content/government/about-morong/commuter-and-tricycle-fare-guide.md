This guide provides practical public transit information for commuters traveling to, from, and within the **Municipality of Morong, Province of Rizal**, covering regional transit corridors, statutory fare discounts, local tricycle (TODA) regulations, and consumer rights.

## 2. Statutory 20% Fare Discounts (By Law)

Under Philippine national legislation, public utility vehicle (PUV) drivers and operators—including jeepneys, UV Express, public buses, and **tricycles**—are legally required to grant a **20% fare discount** to eligible passengers:

### Who is Entitled to the 20% Discount?

1. **Students (RA 11314 — Student Fare Discount Act)**:
   - Covers all Filipino students enrolled in basic education (elementary/high school), senior high school, vocational, and undergraduate tertiary programs.
   - **Valid 365 days a year**, including weekends, summer breaks, and official holidays.
   - **Requirement**: Present a valid school ID issued for the current school year or validated enrollment receipt.
2. **Senior Citizens (RA 9994 — Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010)**:
   - Filipino citizens aged sixty (60) years old or older.
   - **Requirement**: Present an official Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) ID or valid government ID indicating date of birth.
3. **Persons with Disabilities (RA 10754 — Expanded Rights and Privileges of PWDs)**:
   - Any individual with physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory impairments.
   - **Requirement**: Present an official PWD ID issued by the Morong MSWDO / PDAO or National Council on Disability Affairs (NCDA).

## 3. Morong Tricycle (TODA) Network & Zones

Tricycles are the primary mode of last-mile local transportation across Morong's 8 barangays. Each tricycle belongs to a designated **Tricycle Operators and Drivers Association (TODA)** authorized by the **Municipal Tricycle Franchising and Regulatory Board (MTFRB)** of Morong.

### Major TODA Terminals Across the 8 Barangays

- **Poblacion & Market TODA**: Serving commercial centers, Town Plaza, Morong Public Market, St. Jerome Church, and municipal offices in Brgy. San Pedro, San Jose, and San Juan.
- **San Juan – Paglabas TODA**: Serving New Municipal Building (Sitio Paglabas), RPHS Morong Main hospital, and residential communities in San Juan.
- **Lagundi TODA**: Serving Manila East Road highway junctions, residential subdivisions, and upland sitios in Brgy. Lagundi.
- **Bombongan TODA**: Connecting interior agricultural communities and riverside residential sitios to the highway.
- **San Guillermo TODA**: Transporting commuters between the San Guillermo parish area and the town proper.
- **Maybancal TODA**: Connecting Maybancal communities, schools, and commercial establishments.
- **Caniogan-Calero-Lanang (CCL) TODA**: Serving coastal Laguna de Bay barangay clusters and lakeside fish landing areas.

### Official Fare Matrix Rules

- **Regular Fare vs. Special Trip**:
  - **Regular / Shared Trip**: Fare charged per passenger when sharing the tricycle cab along designated route lines.
  - **Special Trip**: Direct chartered trip where a single passenger or group charters the entire vehicle to a specific drop-off destination.
- **Fare Matrix Display**: By municipal regulation, every authorized tricycle cab must visibly display the official stamped **Municipal Tricycle Fare Matrix** inside the vehicle for passenger verification.

## 4. How to Report Overcharging or Refusal to Convey

Commuters have the right to fair, legal fares and courteous public service. If a driver overcharges, refuses to grant statutory discounts, or refuses conveyance:

### What to Note Down Immediately

1. **Tricycle Body Number**: Large number painted on the front and back of the tricycle passenger sidecar.
2. **Plate Number / MV File Number**: LTO registration plate on the motorcycle.
3. **TODA Sticker / Association Name**: Displayed on the windshield or side of the sidecar.
4. **Incident Details**: Exact date, time, pickup point, drop-off destination, and amount demanded.

### Where to File a Complaint

1. **Step 1: File with the Municipal Franchising Board**:
   - Office: **Municipal Tricycle Franchising and Regulatory Board (MTFRB) / Sangguniang Bayan Committee on Transportation**
   - Location: 2nd Floor, New Municipal Building, Sitio Paglabas, Morong, Rizal
   - Phone: (02) 7212-5741
2. **Step 2: Assistance from Morong PNP Traffic Division**:
   - If an on-the-spot confrontation or safety issue arises, proceed to the **Morong Municipal Police Station (PNP)** near the Municipal Hall compound or call **0906-513-0718** / **0998-598-5725**.
3. **Step 3: Barangay Grievance Desk**:
   - You may also lodge a grievance at the Barangay Hall where the TODA terminal is anchored.
