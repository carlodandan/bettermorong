# Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction & Management Office (MDRRMO) — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction & Management Office (MDRRMO)** operates the 24/7 Morong Emergency Operations Center, leading disaster prevention, mitigation, emergency preparedness, search and rescue, and disaster response for the entire municipality.

## Core Operations

### 1. 24/7 Emergency Dispatch & Medical Response

- Standby rapid emergency medical response teams and equipped ambulances for vehicular accidents, medical trauma, and emergency hospital transport.
- Dispatch coordination with Rizal Provincial Disaster Office and neighboring LGUs (Cardona, Baras, Binangonan, Tanay).

### 2. River Level & Weather Early Warning System

- Continuous real-time water level monitoring along the **Morong River**, Laguna de Bay lakeshore areas, and low-lying barangays (e.g., San Juan, San Pedro, Maybancal).
- Timely dissemination of PAGASA weather bulletins, rainfall warnings, and localized evacuation advisories.

### 3. Community Training & Barangay Resilience

- Training of Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Committees (BDRRMCs).
- Standard First Aid (SFA) and Basic Life Support (BLS) certification seminars for schools, barangay responders, and commercial institutions.
- Multi-hazard drills (Earthquake, Flood, Fire evacuation) conducted across schools and municipal buildings.
