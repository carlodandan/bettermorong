# Municipal Budget Office — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Budget Office** administers the fiscal planning, annual investment budgeting, budget execution, and expenditure control of the Municipal Government of Morong in strict accordance with the Local Government Code of 1991 (RA 7160) and Department of Budget and Management (DBM) circulars.

## Frontline Services (Citizen's Charter 2025)

### 1. Issuance of Certificate of Appropriation Balances

- **Service Classification:** Simple (10 to 30 minutes)
- **Transaction Type:** G2G (Internal Government Service)
- **Scope:** Verifies the availability of municipal budget appropriations for purchase orders, infrastructure contracts, barangay financial assistance, and employee benefits before fund obligations are encumbered.

### 2. Cancelation of Obligations & Budget Realignment Review

- **Service Classification:** Simple (15 to 30 minutes)
- **Scope:** Processes cancellation of unexpended purchase requests or contracts, releasing budgetary allotments back to active municipal program balances.
