# Municipal Environment & Natural Resources Office (MENRO) — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)** oversees environmental protection, ecological solid waste management, waterway conservation, and natural resources sustainability across the Municipality of Morong, Rizal.

## Core Environmental Programs

### 1. Ecological Solid Waste Management (RA 9003)

- **Garbage Collection Schedules**: Monitoring municipal waste collection trucks servicing the 8 barangays of Morong.
- **Waste Segregation**: Enforcing mandatory source segregation into biodegradable, recyclable, residual, and special/hazardous household wastes.
- **Municipal Garbage Collection Fees**: Implementing approved municipal ordinances for residential and commercial solid waste management service charges.
- **Materials Recovery Facilities (MRF)**: Coordination with barangay MRFs for community recycling and upcycling.

### 2. Water Quality & Septage Management

- **Septage Management Ordinance**: Regulation and monitoring of residential and commercial septic tank desludging every 3 to 5 years by accredited service providers to protect groundwater and Laguna de Bay.
- **Morong River Clean-up & Protection**: Regular clean-up operations, riverbank clearing, and water quality testing of tributaries flowing into Laguna de Bay.

### 3. Tree Preservation & Forestry Management

- **Tree Trimming / Cutting Evaluation**: Site inspection and recommendation for tree trimming, cutting, or balling permits pursuant to the Municipal Tree Preservation Ordinance and DENR-CENRO guidelines.
- **Greening & Reforestation**: Community tree planting drives in watershed and upland zones of Morong.

### 4. Environmental Clearances & Inspections

- Issuance of **MENRO Environmental Clearance** for business permits and commercial developments.
- Ocular inspection of poultry, piggeries, and commercial facilities for smoke, odor, wastewater, and noise compliance.
