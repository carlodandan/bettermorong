# Office of the Municipal Accountant — Morong, Rizal

The **Office of the Municipal Accountant** maintains the official books of accounts, financial reporting, pre-audit verifications, and financial statement consolidation of the Municipal Government of Morong in compliance with the Commission on Audit (COA) Government Accounting Manual (GAM).

## Frontline Services (Citizen's Charter 2025)

### 1. Issuance of Certificate of Premium Contribution and/or Certificate of Loan Payment

- **Service Classification:** Simple (10 to 20 minutes)
- **Transaction Type:** G2C / G2G
- **Eligibility:** Active and former municipal employees, elected officials, and contract-of-service personnel.
- **Scope:** Verification and certification of GSIS, Pag-IBIG (HDMF), PhilHealth, and Landbank salary loan deductions and official remit dates for loan clearance, terminal leave, or retirement filing.
