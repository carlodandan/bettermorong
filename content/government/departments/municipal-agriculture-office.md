# Municipal Agriculture Office — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)** supports and empowers local farmers, fisherfolk, and livestock raisers across Morong through technical assistance, farm input subsidies, livestock health programs, and sustainable fishery management along Laguna de Bay.

## Programs and Services

### 1. Crop Production & Farmers Support

- **Certified Seeds & Seedlings**: Distribution of high-yield rice, corn, and vegetable seeds provided by the Department of Agriculture (DA) and municipal funds.
- **Organic Fertilizer & Soil Health**: Assistance with organic compost, bio-fertilizers, and soil sampling analysis.
- **Pest & Disease Surveillance**: Technical field diagnosis, pest management guidance, and farm machinery consultation.
- **Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA)**: Official registration of Morong farmers to access government agricultural subsidies and crop insurance.

### 2. Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Management

- **Fisherfolk Registration (FishR)**: Registration of local fisherfolk operating in coastal barangays fronting Laguna de Bay.
- **Fishing Vessel Registration (BoatR)**: Compliance inspection and registration of motorized and non-motorized fishing bancas (3 gross tons and below) pursuant to municipal fishery ordinances.
- **Fisheries Management Coordination**: Partnering with the Municipal Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Management Council (MFARMC) for water conservation and anti-illegal fishing enforcement.

### 3. Veterinary & Livestock Services

- **Livestock Deworming & Vitamins**: Regular animal healthcare services for cattle, carabaos, goats, and pigs.
- **Free Anti-Rabies Vaccination**: Regular pet vaccination campaigns for dogs and cats across the 8 barangays of Morong.
- **Livestock Dispersal & Upgrading**: Coordination with provincial veterinary services for breeding improvement and disease prevention (e.g., African Swine Fever surveillance).
