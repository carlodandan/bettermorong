## Executive Mandate and Functions

Under Republic Act No. 7160 (The Local Government Code of 1991), the Municipal Mayor exercises general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the municipal government:

1. **Policy Execution**: Enforces all laws and ordinances relative to the governance of the municipality and the exercise of its corporate powers.
2. **Citizen Frontline Services**: Directs the formulation of the municipal development plan with the Municipal Development Council and ensures frontline services reach all eight barangays.
3. **Disaster and Emergency Response**: Acts as Chairperson of the Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (MDRRMC), directing disaster preparedness, relief, and rehabilitation operations.
4. **Community Collaboration**: Coordinates with national government agencies, barangay councils, civil society organizations, and local business sectors.

## Frontline Executive Services

### 1. Mayor's Action and Citizen Assistance

Residents in need of medical referrals, indigent assistance, endorsement letters, or civic guidance may approach the Mayor's Office staff for prompt assistance.

### 2. Endorsement for National Government Assistance

Assistance with referrals to national government offices (DSWD, DOH, PCSO, DOLE, TESDA) for specialized medical or educational grants.

### 3. Solemnization of Marriages

Couples seeking civil wedding rites officiated by the Municipal Mayor can file applications via the Local Civil Registry Office and schedule ceremonies through the Mayor’s Office.

### 4. Permitting and Executive Clearances

Issuance of Mayor's Permits for business operations, public events, motorcade permits, and promotional activities in coordination with relevant municipal offices.
