# Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO) — Morong, Rizal

The **Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)**, operating under the Executive Office of the Municipal Mayor, administers local business registration, the annual Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS), regulatory inspection clearances, and the issuance of Mayor's Business and Occupational Working Permits across Morong, Rizal.

## Core Mandates & Functions

In compliance with Republic Act No. 11032 (Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018), the Morong BPLO streamlines commercial transactions:

### 1. New Business Permit Registration

Processes business applications for sole proprietorships, partnerships, corporations, and cooperatives locating operations within the 8 barangays of Morong.

### 2. Annual Business Permit Renewal & BOSS

Coordinates the annual **Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS)** held every January, bringing together the BPLO, Municipal Treasurer, Municipal Health Office (Sanitary), Bureau of Fire Protection (FSIC), MPDO (Zoning), and MENRO to allow business owners to complete compliance and tax assessment in a single streamlined session.

### 3. Individual Mayor’s Occupational Working Permits

Issues working permits and employment IDs for private sector employees, store personnel, sales agents, service staff, barbers, and transport workers operating within municipal borders.

### 4. Regulatory Inspections & Joint Assessment

Coordinates the Joint Inspection Team (JIT) to ensure commercial establishments adhere to the National Building Code, Fire Code of the Philippines, Sanitation Code, and Morong Municipal Revenue ordinances.

### 5. Retirement & Cessation of Business

Processes verified applications for business closure, tax retirement assessments, and the release of Certificates of Retirement of Business.
