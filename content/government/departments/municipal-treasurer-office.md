# Office of the Municipal Treasurer — Morong, Rizal

The **Office of the Municipal Treasurer** is the primary financial administration and revenue-generating arm of the Municipal Government of Morong, responsible for the custody, assessment, collection, and prudent disbursement of all local government funds and taxes.

## Frontline Revenue Services

### 1. Real Property Tax (RPT / Amilyar) Payment

- Assessment of annual property taxes for residential, agricultural, and commercial lands, buildings, and machinery situated within Morong.
- Discounts for prompt and early full payments (up to 20% discount if paid during prompt payment discount periods set by the municipal revenue code).
- Issuance of **Official Receipts** and **Real Property Tax Clearances**.

### 2. Business Tax Collection & Mayor’s Permit Fees

- Assessment and collection of local business taxes, regulatory inspection fees, and sanitary fees during the annual Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS) in January and throughout the fiscal year.
- Collection of quarterly business tax installments.

### 3. Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)

- Issuance of individual and corporate Community Tax Certificates (Cedula) required for notarizations, property transactions, employment, and business permits.

### 4. Regulatory Fees, Clearances & Fines

- Collection of Civil Registry fees (marriage license, certified true copy certificates).
- Building and engineering permit fees.
- Police and Mayor’s clearance fees.
- Morong public market stall rental collections and municipal ordinance violation fines.
