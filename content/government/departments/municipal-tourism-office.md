# Municipal Tourism Office — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Tourism Office** promotes the cultural heritage, eco-tourism destinations, homegrown culinary traditions, and vibrant artistic community of the Municipality of Morong, Rizal. It serves as the municipal liaison for Department of Tourism (DOT) accreditations, tour operators, media productions, and domestic travelers.

## Frontline Services (Citizen's Charter 2025)

### 1. Research, Interview, and Public Information Assistance

- **Service Classification:** Simple (Immediate to 1 working day)
- **Eligibility:** Students, historians, researchers, media organizations, and travel authors.
- **Scope:** Access to local historical archives, Saint Jerome Parish Church documentation, cultural masterplans, and municipal tourism statistics.

### 2. Request for Media Features and Municipal Facility Filming

- **Service Classification:** Simple (1 to 2 working days)
- **Scope:** Clearances for commercial photography, television broadcasting, documentaries, and vlog shoots at the historic Morong Town Plaza, Saint Jerome belfry exterior, and municipal eco-parks.

### 3. Tour Coordination and Group Visitor Assistance

- **Service Classification:** Simple (3 working days advance notice)
- **Scope:** Itinerary coordination, local DOT-accredited tour guide matching, and visitor security coordination with local barangays for heritage walks, farm staycations, and mountain trails.
