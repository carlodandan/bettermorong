# Local Civil Registry Office — Morong, Rizal

The **Local Civil Registry Office (LCRO)** is responsible for the systematic civil registration of vital events — births, marriages, and deaths — and documents affecting the civil status of persons in the Municipality of Morong, Rizal.

## Core Services

### 1. Registration of Vital Events

- **Birth Registration (Timely & Delayed)**: Registration of newborns within 30 days of birth (free timely registration) and delayed registration with supporting affidavits.
- **Application for Marriage License**: Processing marriage license applications, verification of legal capacity, and pre-marriage counseling referrals.
- **Registration of Death Certificates**: Official registration and certified true copies of Certificates of Death.
- **Annual Kasalang Bayan**: Facilitation of the annual municipal mass wedding program organized for couples residing in Morong.

### 2. Certified Copies & Endorsement

- **Certified True Copies (CTC)**: Issuance of certified copies of birth, marriage, and death records filed in the Morong local civil registry.
- **PSA Electronic Endorsement**: Fast-tracking the endorsement of local records to the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) central database for PSA security paper issuance.

### 3. Legal Instruments & Corrections

- **Republic Act No. 9048**: Administrative correction of clerical or typographical errors in first names, nicknames, and dates of birth.
- **Republic Act No. 10172**: Administrative correction of gender/sex and date/month of birth.
- **Republic Act No. 9255**: Use of father's surname for illegitimate children (Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father - AUSF).
- **Legitimation and Court Decrees**: Registration of legitimations through subsequent marriage, adoptions, and court annulments.

---

## General Requirements for Vital Records

1. Valid government-issued identification of the document owner or authorized representative.
2. If requesting through a representative: Signed Authorization Letter and photocopy of the ID of both the owner and representative.
3. Relevant supporting documents (e.g., Baptismal Certificate, Form 137/school records, voter certification) for delayed registrations or corrections.
