# Public Employment Service Office (PESO) — Morong, Rizal

The **Public Employment Service Office (PESO)** of Morong, Rizal is a multi-service facility established pursuant to Republic Act No. 8759 (PESO Act of 1999) as amended by RA 10691, operating in partnership with the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) to provide comprehensive employment facilitation, youth career programs, and labor welfare support to Morong residents.

## Core Employment Programs & Services

### 1. Job Referral, Placement & Megajob Fairs

- **PhilJobNet / Local Job Registry**: Registration of active jobseekers and matching with accredited corporate, manufacturing, and commercial employers in Morong, Rizal province, and neighboring economic zones.
- **Municipal Job Fairs**: Regularly scheduled job fairs bringing direct hiring companies and recruitment agencies to Sitio Paglabas for on-the-spot interviews.

### 2. Special Program for the Employment of Students (SPES)

- Provides temporary short-term employment to underprivileged but deserving high school, senior high school, vocational, and tertiary students during summer and semestral breaks.
- Student beneficiaries earn statutory wages (funded 60% by the Municipal Government of Morong and 40% by DOLE via educational vouchers) to support their tuition and school expenses.

### 3. Government Internship Program (GIP)

- Offers recent college and technical-vocational graduates aged 18 to 30 an opportunity to gain 3 to 6 months of paid civil service experience within various municipal departments and barangay units in Morong.

### 4. DOLE TUPAD & Emergency Employment Coordination

- Serves as the municipal coordinating desk for the **Tulong Panghanapbuhay sa Ating Disadvantaged/Displaced Workers (TUPAD)** program, providing emergency wage employment for displaced, underemployed, and seasonal informal workers engaged in community cleaning, disaster rehabilitation, and greening projects.

### 5. OFW Help Desk & Reintegration Assistance

- Support desk for Morong Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) and their families in coordination with the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) and Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA).
- Facilitates livelihood loan programs, skills retraining, and reintegration grants for returning overseas workers.
