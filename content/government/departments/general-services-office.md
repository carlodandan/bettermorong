# General Services Office (GSO) — Morong, Rizal

The **General Services Office (GSO)** is the logistical, asset management, and property administration arm of the Municipal Government of Morong. It oversees the custody, maintenance, insurance, physical security, and public rental of all municipal properties, heavy equipment, and public infrastructure.

## Frontline Services (Citizen's Charter 2025)

### 1. Rental of Personal and Real Properties Owned by the Municipality

- **Service Classification:** Simple (1 to 2 working days)
- **Transaction Type:** G2C / G2B (Government to Citizen / Business)
- **Covered Facilities & Equipment:**
  - Municipal gymnasiums, sports complexes, and open plazas for private, civic, or commercial gatherings.
  - Sound systems, chairs, tables, tents, and stage equipment.
  - Public utility stalls and municipal event logistics.
- **Requirements:**
  1. Formal Letter of Intent addressed to the Municipal Mayor (recommending approval from GSO).
  2. Proof of identification of the requesting organization or party.
  3. Official payment of prescribed rental fee at the Municipal Treasury.

### 2. Municipal Property Asset Tracking & Logistics Support

- Physical inventory and asset tagging of government property under COA regulations.
- Support during public emergencies, relief distributions, and town fiestas.
