# Municipal Health Office (MHO) — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Health Office (MHO)** provides comprehensive primary healthcare, preventative programs, maternal and child care, disease surveillance, and emergency clinical services to all residents of Morong, Rizal.

## Weekly Clinic Schedule & Services

The Rural Health Unit adheres to a regular weekly schedule for specialized clinical services:

| Service / Program                          | Schedule                           | Description                                                                                                               |
| :----------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **General Consultation & Checkup**         | Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM | Free outpatient medical consultations, diagnosis, and prescription of essential medicines.                                |
| **Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI)** | Wednesdays                         | Free routine vaccination for infants and toddlers (BCG, Hepatitis B, Pentavalent, OPV/IPV, PCV, MMR).                     |
| **Prenatal & Family Planning Services**    | Tuesdays                           | Prenatal checkups for expectant mothers, pap smear screening, tetanus toxoid vaccination, and family planning counseling. |
| **Newborn Screening (NBS)**                | Monday – Thursday                  | Early detection of metabolic and congenital disorders for newborns within 24 to 72 hours of birth.                        |
| **Animal Bite Treatment Center (ABTC)**    | Mon – Fri (Urgent cases)           | Assessment, rabies prophylaxis injection, and post-exposure treatment for dog/cat bites and scratches.                    |
| **TB-DOTS Clinic**                         | Monday – Friday                    | Directly Observed Therapy Short-Course for tuberculosis diagnosis (GeneXpert / sputum test) and free medication regimen.  |
| **Dental Health Services**                 | Designated clinic days             | Basic dental exams, tooth extractions, and oral health guidance for students and indigent residents.                      |
| **Sanitary Inspection & Health Cards**     | Monday – Friday                    | Issuance of Sanitary Permits for commercial establishments and Health Certificates for food handlers and workers.         |

---

## What to Bring for Checkups

- **Valid Government ID** or **Barangay Certificate of Residency** (for Morong residents)
- **PhilHealth Member ID / MDR** (if registered member or dependent)
- **Child Health & Immunization Record Card (EPI Booklet)** for infant vaccinations
- **Previous medical records, laboratory results, or prescriptions** if available
