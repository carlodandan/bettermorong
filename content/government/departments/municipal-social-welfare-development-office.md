# Municipal Social Welfare & Development Office (MSWDO) — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Social Welfare & Development Office (MSWDO)** provides social protection, crisis intervention, and specialized welfare services to marginalized individuals, families, senior citizens, persons with disabilities (PWDs), solo parents, and vulnerable sectors in Morong.

## Key Assistance Programs

### 1. Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS)

Immediate financial and material assistance for indigent individuals and families experiencing crisis:

- **Medical Assistance**: Support for hospital bills, diagnostic laboratory procedures, and maintenance medicines.
- **Burial Assistance**: Funeral and burial financial support for deceased indigent residents.
- **Educational Assistance**: One-time financial support for disadvantaged students.
- **Transportation Assistance**: Financial aid for stranded individuals returning to their home provinces.

### 2. Senior Citizens Services (OSCA Coordination)

- Application and issuance of **Senior Citizen ID** and Medicine/Grocery Discount Purchase Booklets.
- Social pension coordination for qualified indigent elderly citizens.
- Centenarian incentive processing under national and local ordinances.

### 3. Persons with Disability (PWD) Services

- Verification and issuance of **Persons with Disabilities (PWD) ID Cards**.
- Assistive device distribution (wheelchairs, walking canes, hearing aids) when available.
- Facilitation of Republic Act No. 10754 20% discounts and VAT exemptions.

### 4. Solo Parents Welfare (RA 11861)

- Screening and issuance of **Solo Parent ID** cards and booklets.
- Livelihood training referrals and educational assistance priority for eligible children of solo parents.

### 5. Child & Youth Welfare and Family Protection

- Supervision of Barangay Early Childhood Care and Development (Daycare) centers.
- Case management and psychosocial support for women and children in need of special protection (VAWC and CICL).
- Collaboration with the Local Council for the Protection of Children (LCPC) and LCAT-VAWC.
