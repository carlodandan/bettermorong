# Office of the Municipal Assessor — Morong, Rizal

The **Office of the Municipal Assessor** establishes and maintains a systematic method of real property assessment and appraisal for taxation purposes in Morong, ensuring equitable valuation of lands, buildings, and improvements across the municipality.

## Core Services & Procedures

### 1. Issuance of Tax Declaration (Transfer of Ownership)

For newly acquired properties via Deed of Absolute Sale, Donation, or Extrajudicial Settlement:

- **Requirements**:
  - Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT / OCT) certified by the Registry of Deeds.
  - Notarized Deed of Sale / Donation / Settlement document.
  - Certificate Authorizing Registration (eCAR) from the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR).
  - Real Property Tax Clearance and Transfer Tax receipt from the Municipal Treasurer.
  - Previous Tax Declaration copy.

### 2. Certified Copies of Documents

- Issuance of **Certified True Copies of Tax Declaration** for land, building, or machinery.
- **Certificate of Property Holdings / Non-Holding** (often requested for scholarships, public assistance, or court filings).
- **Certificate of No Improvement / With Improvement** (needed for construction and building permits).

### 3. Property Assessment & Re-assessment

- Ocular inspection and reassessment of properties due to structural renovation, destruction, change in actual use (e.g., residential to commercial), or subdivision of lots.
- Cadastral map referencing and tax mapping index verification.
