# Human Resource Management Office (HRMO) — Morong, Rizal

The **Human Resource Management Office (HRMO)** directs the personnel development, recruitment, merit selection, employee welfare, and public service capacity building for the Municipal Government of Morong in accordance with Civil Service Commission (CSC) laws and rules.

## Frontline Services (Citizen's Charter 2025)

### 1. Job Applications for Plantilla (Permanent) Positions

- **Service Classification:** Complex (Evaluated by the Human Resource Merit Promotion and Selection Board - HRMPSB)
- **Requirements:**
  1. Personal Data Sheet (CSC Form 212 Revised 2017) with recent passport-size picture.
  2. Authenticated Certificate of Eligibility (Civil Service Professional / Sub-Professional or PRC Board License).
  3. Transcript of Records (TOR) and Diploma.
  4. Performance Ratings (IPCR for government applicants).

### 2. Job Applications for Job Order / Contractual Positions

- **Service Classification:** Simple (3 to 5 working days)
- **Requirements:** Resume / Biodata, Barangay Clearance, and relevant trade certifications (TESDA, NC II if applicable).

### 3. Student Work Immersion & Internship Programs

- **Senior High School Work Immersion:** Coordination with DepEd-accredited secondary schools in Morong for administrative and technical office immersion.
- **College On-the-Job Training (OJT):** Endorsement from higher education institutions (HEIs) for practicum placements across municipal engineering, health, planning, and accounting departments.

### 4. Civil Service Examination Client Assistance & Non-Employment Certificates

- Distribution of CSC exam application notices and requirements guidance.
- Issuance of Certificates of Non-Employment / Unemployment for indigent scholarship and legal assistance applicants.
