# Office of the Municipal Engineer — Morong, Rizal

The **Office of the Municipal Engineer** (serving concurrently as the **Office of the Building Official**) administers the National Building Code of the Philippines (PD 1096) and oversees the planning, construction, and maintenance of public infrastructure and facilities across the Municipality of Morong.

## Frontline Permits & Engineering Services

### 1. Building Permit Application

Required before erecting, constructing, altering, repairing, or moving any building or structure in Morong:

- **Required Documents**:
  - Five (5) sets of complete Architectural, Structural, Electrical, Sanitary, and Mechanical plans signed and sealed by duly licensed Filipino professionals.
  - Certified true copy of Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) or Deed of Sale / Consent of owner.
  - Real Property Tax Clearance and latest Tax Declaration.
  - Locational Clearance / Zoning Clearance from the MPDO.
  - Barangay Construction Clearance.
  - Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) from the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP).

### 2. Ancillary & Ancillary Building Permits

- **Fencing Permit**: For perimeter fencing and boundary walls.
- **Electrical Permit & Certificate of Final Electrical Inspection (CFEI)**: Required for power energization with Meralco.
- **Demolition Permit**: Required prior to tearing down any existing structure.
- **Signage & Billboard Permit**: For outdoor commercial signage and advertising structures.

### 3. Certificate of Occupancy

- Issued after final ocular inspection verifying that the structure was built strictly in compliance with approved building plans and safety codes.

### 4. Public Works & Infrastructure Maintenance

- Road rehabilitation, drainage maintenance, and desilting along municipal waterways.
- Maintenance of municipal hall buildings, town plaza, evacuation centers, and health facilities.
