# Sangguniang Bayan — Municipality of Morong, Rizal

The **Sangguniang Bayan** is the legislative body of the Municipality of Morong, Province of Rizal, mandated to enact ordinances, approve resolutions, and allocate public funds for the general welfare and development of the municipality and its constituents pursuant to Republic Act No. 7160 (The Local Government Code of 1991).

## Powers and Functions

Pursuant to Section 16 and Section 22 of RA 7160, the Sangguniang Bayan performs the following:

1. **Enact Ordinances and Approve Resolutions**: Legislate measures necessary for efficient and effective municipal governance and the promotion of the general welfare.
2. **Resource & Revenue Maximization**: Formulate tax ordinances, set regulatory fees, and approve annual investment programs and municipal executive budgets.
3. **Franchises & Regulatory Permits**: Grant franchises and regulate municipal public transportation, commercial activities, and zoning compliances.
4. **Land Use & Building Regulation**: Regulate physical planning and building structures within the municipality in accordance with approved zoning ordinances.
5. **Basic Services & Public Infrastructure**: Authorize public works, disaster risk reduction funding, and social service programs for Morong's 8 barangays.

## Citizen Participation

- **Regular Sessions**: Convened weekly at the Legislative Session Hall. Open to the public.
- **Public Hearings**: Scheduled per proposed ordinance to collect citizen and stakeholder input before final deliberation.
- **Filing Petitions & Communications**: Submit letters addressed to the Office of the Sangguniang Bayan Secretary, 2nd Floor, Municipal Building.
