## Frontline Services & Clearances

### 1. Locational / Zoning Clearance

Required for all building permit applications and business permit applications:

- **Zoning Conformity**: Verifies whether a proposed construction or commercial activity conforms to Morong's **Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP)** and Municipal Zoning Ordinance.
- **Documents Required**:
  - Application Form for Locational Clearance.
  - Site Development Plan and Vicinity Map.
  - Proof of land ownership (Title, Deed of Sale, or Lease Contract).
  - Barangay Business Clearance (for businesses) or Barangay Construction Clearance.
  - Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) or Certificate of Non-Coverage (CNC) from DENR-EMB (for qualifying industries).

### 2. Comprehensive Development Plans & Investment Programs

- Formulation of the **Comprehensive Development Plan (CDP)** and **Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP)**.
- Preparation of the annual **Annual Investment Program (AIP)** and Local Development Investment Program (LDIP) in coordination with the Municipal Development Council (MDC).

### 3. Socio-Economic Profile & Geographic Data

- Custodian of municipal statistics, demographic data, and thematic maps of Morong's 8 barangays.
- Providing statistical data and research materials for students, investors, and national government agencies.
