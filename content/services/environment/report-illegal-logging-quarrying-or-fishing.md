# Report Illegal Logging, Quarrying, or Fishing — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipality of Morong** strictly enforces national environmental laws and local municipal ordinances against environmental degradation, illegal tree cutting, unauthorized gravel extraction, and destructive fishing methods within its municipal boundaries and adjacent waters of Laguna de Bay.

---

## Prohibited Environmental Violations

### 1. Illegal Logging & Unauthorized Tree Cutting:

- Cutting of indigenous or planted trees on public or private land without a valid Tree Cutting Permit from DENR and endorsement from MENRO, in accordance with **Municipal Ordinance No. 8, Series of 2017** (Tree Preservation and Environmental Protection).
- Slash-and-burn farming (_kaingin_) in upland mountain areas.

### 2. Illegal Quarrying & Earth Extraction:

- Unpermitted sand, gravel, or soil quarrying along riverbeds, creek embankments, or mountain slopes without a Provincial Quarry Permit and Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC).
- Earth-moving activities causing soil erosion, landslides, or disruption of natural water channels.

### 3. Destructive & Illegal Fishing:

- Electro-fishing, dynamite fishing, and chemical poisoning in Laguna de Bay or local rivers.
- Use of fine-mesh nets or motorized push nets (_sudsod_) that trap fingerlings and destroy aquatic breeding beds, violating the Philippine Fisheries Code (RA 8550 as amended).
- Operating unregistered commercial fishing boats within municipal municipal waters.

---

## How to Report a Violation

To ensure prompt investigation while protecting reporter confidentiality, report incidents through any of the following channels:

1. **MENRO Environmental Hotline & Office**:
   - Proceed to the **Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office**, Ground Floor, Morong Municipal Hall, San Pedro.
   - Or file a report through the **Municipal Administration Office**: `(02) 8786-9398`.
2. **PNP Morong Environmental Desk & Police Hotline**:
   - Contact PNP Morong for immediate field apprehension: `0906-513-0718` / `0998-598-5725`.
3. **Barangay Environmental Desk**:
   - File an incident report with the Barangay Tanod or Punong Barangay where the violation is occurring.
4. **Emergency / Disaster Desk**:
   - For activities causing flash flood or landslide hazards, alert the **24/7 MDRRMO Hotline**: `0917-135-0541`.

---

## Required Details When Reporting:

- Specific location (Barangay, Sitio, landmark, riverbank, or lake coordinates).
- Nature of activity (tree cutting, backhoe excavation, fishing gear type).
- Date, time, and photos or video evidence (if safely obtainable).
- Vehicle license plates or boat markings involved.
