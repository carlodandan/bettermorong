# Clean-Up Drives & Tree Planting Activities — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)** of Morong spearheads environmental stewardship programs, community tree-planting initiatives, and waterway rehabilitation drives to protect the municipal ecological landscape—from the Sierra Madre foothills down to the shores of Laguna de Bay.

---

## 1. Community Waterway Clean-Up Drives

Morong is traversed by vital freshwater rivers and creeks that require ongoing protection against plastic pollution and siltation:

- **Morong River & Talaga Creek Rehabilitation**: Regular clearing of water hyacinths, household debris, and accumulated silt to maintain free hydraulic flow and prevent urban flooding.
- **Laguna de Bay Lakeshore Clean-Up**: Monthly coastal cleanups in lakeshore barangays (Bombongan, CCL, San Juan, Lagundi) in partnership with the Laguna Lake Development Authority (LLDA).
- **International Coastal Clean-Up & Earth Day**: Town-wide volunteer mobilization engaging youth, public school students, civil society organizations, and local business establishments.

---

## 2. Tree Planting & Sierra Madre Reforestation

- **Upland Reforestation**: Planting of endemic timber and fruit-bearing trees (Narrah, Molave, Mahogany, Rambutan, and Cacao) in the rolling hills of Barangay San Guillermo and Barangay Bombongan.
- **Riverbank Riparian Greening**: Bamboo and vetiver grass planting along river embankments to stabilize soil and prevent riverbank erosion.
- **Urban Greening**: Tree planting along school perimeters, municipal parks, and barangay open spaces.

---

## How to Volunteer or Organize a Partner Drive

1. **For Individuals & Youth Groups**:
   - Register as an eco-volunteer at the MENRO office or your local Barangay Council on Environment.
   - Watch for scheduled clean-up drives announced on the BetterMorong portal.
2. **For Schools, Companies & Civic Organizations**:
   - Submit an Activity Coordination Letter to the **MENRO Officer** detailing proposed dates, volunteer count, and preferred tree planting or clean-up site.
   - MENRO provides planting saplings, technical guidance on tree spacing, and post-activity waste collection.
