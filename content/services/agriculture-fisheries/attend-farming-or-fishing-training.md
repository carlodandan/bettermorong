# Farming & Fishing Training Programs — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)** organizes specialized seminars and capacity-building workshops for local farmers, fisherfolk, agricultural entrepreneurs, and rural youth to promote sustainable and profitable agriculture in Morong.

---

## Training Programs Offered

1. **Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) & Organic Farming**:
   - Composting, vermiculture, and botanical pest management.
   - Soil fertility enhancement and pesticide safety.
2. **High-Value Crops Production**:
   - Greenhouse and drip-irrigation vegetable farming.
   - Off-season cultivation and post-harvest handling for market vendors.
3. **Freshwater Aquaculture & Sustainable Fishing**:
   - Best practices for tilapia pond and pen management in Laguna de Bay.
   - Fishery conservation, water quality monitoring, and compliance with the Fisheries Code (RA 8550 as amended).
4. **Agri-Business & Product Value-Adding**:
   - Food processing seminars including native Morong delicacies (_Balaw-balaw_ production, fish processing, and fruit preserves).
   - Packaging, labeling, and market linkage with DTI and local bazaars.

---

## Registration & Requirements

- Open to all Morong residents, registered farmers (RSBSA), fisherfolk (FishR), and members of local farmers' cooperatives.
- **Requirements**:
  - Valid Government-issued ID.
  - Barangay Certificate of Residency.
- **Inquiries & Sign-Up**:
  - Visit the **Municipal Agriculture Office**, Ground Floor, New Municipal Building, Sitio Paglabas, San Pedro, Morong, Rizal.
  - Email or coordinate through your Punong Barangay / Committee on Agriculture.
  - **Registration Fee**: **Free** (includes training materials and starter seed kits when sponsored by the LGU or DA).
