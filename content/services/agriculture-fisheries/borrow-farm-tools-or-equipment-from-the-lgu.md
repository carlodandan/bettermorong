# Farm Tools & Equipment Assistance — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipality of Morong**, through the **Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)**, maintains a shared machinery pool to assist registered farmers, agrarian reform beneficiaries, and farming cooperatives with land preparation, irrigation, and post-harvest operations.

---

## Available Equipment & Machinery

1. **Portable Water Pumps & Irrigation Hoses**: Available for borrowing during dry spells and drought emergencies for lowland rice and vegetable plots.
2. **Knapsack Sprayers**: Manual and battery-operated sprayers for pest management and liquid foliar fertilizer application.
3. **Mechanical Hand Tractors / Tillers**: Coordinated through accredited farmers' associations for communal field preparation in Bombongan, San Guillermo, and San Jose.
4. **Post-Harvest Threshers & Moisture Meters**: Grain quality inspection instruments and harvesting support equipment.

---

## Borrowing Procedure

1. **Eligibility**:
   - Must be an active registered farmer in the RSBSA database.
   - Or an accredited local farmers' association / cooperative in Morong.
2. **Application**:
   - Submit a formal Equipment Borrowing Request at the MAO office at least 3 business days prior to intended use.
   - Present a valid ID and indicate the farm location, specific area, and borrowing duration.
3. **Equipment Check-out & Return**:
   - Undergo standard equipment inspection before hand-over.
   - Borrowers are responsible for standard operating fuel and returning equipment in clean, working condition.
