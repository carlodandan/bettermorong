# Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers & Fingerlings — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)** of Morong provides free agricultural and fisheries inputs to registered farmers, fisherfolk, and urban gardeners to enhance food security and agricultural productivity across the municipality's 8 barangays.

---

## Available Agricultural & Fishery Inputs

1. **Certified Rice Seeds**: Inbred and hybrid seed varieties distributed in partnership with the Department of Agriculture (DA RFO IV-A) and PhilRice during wet and dry cropping seasons.
2. **High-Value Vegetable Seeds & Seedlings**: Eggplant, tomato, sitao, okra, ampalaya, and leafy greens for lowland and upland farming in Bombongan, San Guillermo, and San Jose.
3. **Bio-Fertilizers & Soil Conditioners**: Organic fertilizers and soil inoculants distributed to improve soil fertility and reduce production costs.
4. **Freshwater Fingerlings (Tilapia & Bangus)**: Fingerling dispersal for freshwater fish cage and fishpond operators along Laguna de Bay and local tributaries.

---

## Eligibility Requirements

- Registered in the **Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA)** or Morong Fisherfolk Registry (FishR).
- Resident or active farm cultivator/aquaculture operator in Morong, Rizal.
- Valid Government-issued ID and Barangay Certificate of Residency or Farming Certification.

---

## Step-by-Step Request Process

1. **Visit the Municipal Agriculture Office**: Proceed to the Ground Floor, New Municipal Building, Sitio Paglabas, San Pedro, Morong.
2. **Verify RSBSA / FishR Registration**: Present your ID to the MAO desk officer to confirm your active RSBSA reference number.
3. **Fill Out the Input Request Form**: Specify the land area or pond area under cultivation and the type of input requested.
4. **Claim Inputs or Schedule Dispersal**: Claim seeds and fertilizers directly from the MAO seed bank, or receive scheduled batch deliveries organized at your local Barangay Hall.
