# Veterinary Services for Livestock & Farm Animals — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)**, in coordination with the Rizal Provincial Veterinary Office (PVO), provides free preventive veterinary services, livestock immunization, and animal health consultations for livestock raisers in Morong.

---

## Services Offered

1. **Anti-Rabies Vaccination**: Mass animal vaccination for dogs and cats across all 8 barangays in compliance with Republic Act No. 9482 (Anti-Rabies Act).
2. **Hemorrhagic Septicemia (Hemosep) Vaccination**: Immunization for carabaos and cattle to prevent seasonal bacterial respiratory outbreaks.
3. **Deworming & Vitamin Supplementation**: Internal parasite control and injectable multivitamins for swine, goats, sheep, and draft animals.
4. **African Swine Fever (ASF) Biosecurity & Surveillance**: Free farm inspection, biosecurity assessment, and issuance of local animal transport certifications.

---

## How to Avail of Services

1. **Community Animal Health Drives**: Watch for scheduled barangay-by-barangay veterinary missions announced through your Barangay Hall.
2. **Office-Based Consultations & Field Visits**:
   - For farm-level emergencies or herd vaccinations, submit a service request letter at the MAO office.
   - Coordinate with MAO livestock inspectors at the Ground Floor, New Municipal Building.
3. **Animal Health Inquiries**:
   - **Office**: Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)
   - **Location**: Ground Floor, New Municipal Building, Sitio Paglabas, San Pedro, Morong, Rizal
   - **Schedule**: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
   - **Fees**: Basic vaccines and dewormers provided **Free of Charge** by the LGU.
