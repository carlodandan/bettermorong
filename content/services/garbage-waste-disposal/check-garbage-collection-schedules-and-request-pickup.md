# Check Garbage Collection Schedules and Request Pickup — Morong, Rizal

The Municipal Government of Morong, through the **Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)** in coordination with the eight (8) component barangays, enforces regular solid waste collection pursuant to Republic Act No. 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act) and **Municipal Ordinance No. 5, S. 2014** (Municipal Solid Waste Management & Collection Fee Code).

---

## ⚠️ Strictly Enforced: "No Segregation, No Collection"

MENRO garbage truck crews and barangay eco-aides are legally prohibited from collecting mixed or unsorted trash:

1. **Biodegradable / Composting Waste** (kitchen scraps, food leftovers, fruit peelings, garden trimmings): Collected on designated green days or composted in household/barangay MRF compost pits.
2. **Residual Waste** (candy wrappers, single-use plastic sachets, soiled rags, broken ceramics): Packed in securely tied sturdy bags for sanitary landfill disposal.
3. **Recyclable Materials** (PET plastic bottles, aluminum/tin cans, scrap iron, cardboard, newspapers, clean glass bottles): Cleaned, flattened, and sold to local junk shops or surrendered to your Barangay Materials Recovery Facility (MRF).
4. **Special Household & Hazardous Waste** (fluorescent lamps, e-waste, lead-acid batteries, spray canisters): Surrendered during special MENRO collection drives.

## 2. Requesting Special Bulk, Construction & Tree Trimming Pickup

Standard weekly collection trucks cannot accept heavy construction debris or bulky household furniture:

- **Bulky Household Items**: Old sofas, spring mattresses, broken cabinets, and renovation debris.
- **Garden & Farm Trimmings**: Large fallen tree branches and bamboo stumps after typhoons.
- **Electronic Waste (E-waste)**: Defective computer monitors, refrigerators, and television sets.

### How to Request Special Collection

1. **File Request**: Visit your **Barangay Hall** or the **MENRO Office** at the Municipal Building Compound in Sitio Paglabas.
2. **On-Site Volume Assessment**: A MENRO inspector evaluates the estimated cubic meters and truck requirement.
3. **Fee Assessment**: Settle the special hauling fee at the Municipal Treasury (fees vary according to volume and truck trips under Municipal Ordinance No. 5, S. 2014).
4. **Scheduled Pickup**: The MENRO dump truck dispatches on the agreed weekend or off-peak slot.

## 3. Reporting Illegal Dumping, Burning, or Uncollected Waste

Open burning of leaves or plastic (_siga_) is strictly punishable by law under Republic Act No. 9003 and the Clean Air Act (RA 8749):

- **Report Violations or Uncollected Trash**:
  - Contact the **MENRO Environmental Inspection Unit** at **(02) 7212-5741** or email `menro.morongrizal@gmail.com`.
  - Report to your local **Barangay Council / Kagawad on Environment**.
  - Provide photos, date, time, and exact address/landmark.
