# Free Health Services — Municipality of Morong, Rizal

The Municipal Government of Morong provides free **medical check-ups, essential medicines, and routine vaccines** through the **Municipal Health Office (MHO)**, the **Morong Rural Health Unit (RHU)**, and Barangay Health Stations across all 8 barangays.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Proceed to Triage**: Arrive at the Morong RHU (San Pedro) or your local Barangay Health Station. Vital signs (blood pressure, temperature, weight) will be recorded.
2. **Consultation**: Meet with the municipal physician or nurse for diagnosis and clinical evaluation.
3. **Pharmacy Dispensing**: Present your prescription to the RHU Pharmacy window to claim available free medicines.
4. **Vaccinations**: For child immunization, proceed directly on Wednesdays with your baby’s immunization card.
5. **Rabies Treatment**: If bitten or scratched by a mammal (dog, cat), wash the wound immediately with running water and soap for 15 minutes, then report immediately to the Morong RHU ABTC.
