# Get Animal Bite Treatment and Anti-Rabies Vaccine — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipality of Morong** operates an **Animal Bite Treatment Center (ABTC)** at the Morong Rural Health Unit (RHU) in Barangay San Pedro, providing clinical assessment, post-exposure anti-rabies vaccination, and rabies prevention counseling.

---

## ⚠️ Immediate First Aid (What to Do Right Away)

Rabies is 100% fatal once symptoms appear, but it is **100% preventable** with prompt first aid and immediate vaccination:

1. **Wash Thoroughly with Running Water and Soap Immediately**:
   - Wash the wound vigorously under running tap water using soap or detergent for at least **10 to 15 minutes**. This simple step flushes out a significant percentage of the virus.
2. **Apply Antiseptic**:
   - Apply Povidone-Iodine (Betadine) or 70% rubbing alcohol to the wound.
3. **DO NOT Practice Harmful Traditional Remedies**:
   - **Never perform "Tandok"** (suction of blood using animal horns or stones). Tandok causes severe bacterial infection and does not remove rabies virus.
   - **Never apply garlic, tobacco, bleach, vinegar, or acidic substances** to the wound.
   - **Do not cut, scrape, or cauterize the wound.**
4. **Observe the Animal (If Safe to Do So)**:
   - Confine and observe the biting dog or cat for **fourteen (14) calendar days**. Note if the animal exhibits excessive salivation, aggression, disorientation, paralysis, or dies within 14 days.
   - If the animal is wild, stray, has vanished, or dies, notify the attending doctor immediately.

## Exposure Categories & Where to Go

Healthcare providers classify animal exposures into three World Health Organization (WHO) categories:

| Category         | Type of Exposure                                                                                                                               | Required Medical Action                                                                                  | Facility in Morong                                                                          |
| :--------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Category I**   | Touching or feeding animals; licks on intact, unbroken skin                                                                                    | Wash skin; no anti-rabies vaccine required                                                               | Home first aid                                                                              |
| **Category II**  | Minor scratches or abrasions without bleeding; nibbling on uncovered skin                                                                      | Immediate wound cleaning + Anti-Rabies Vaccine (Active Immunization)                                     | **Morong RHU (Animal Bite Treatment Center)**                                               |
| **Category III** | Transdermal bites or scratches (punctures with bleeding); licks on broken skin; mucous membrane contact (eyes, lips); any bite by bats/monkeys | Immediate wound cleaning + Anti-Rabies Vaccine + **Rabies Immune Globulin (RIG)** (Passive Immunization) | **Morong RHU** (Initial triage) / **Rizal Provincial Hospital System (RPHS - Morong Main)** |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you have severe, deep, bleeding bites (Category III) or bites on the head, neck, face, or hands, you require **Rabies Immune Globulin (RIG)** in addition to the standard vaccine. You will be evaluated at the RHU and, if necessary, referred directly to **Rizal Provincial Hospital System (RPHS - Morong Main)** in Brgy. San Juan.

## Step-by-Step Procedure at the Animal Bite Treatment Center

1. **Immediate Triage & Assessment**: Register at the Morong RHU ABTC counter for wound evaluation and exposure categorization.
2. **Day 0 Injections**: Receive your initial dose of Tetanus Toxoid and Anti-Rabies Vaccine (intradermal injection).
3. **Vaccination Card Issuance**: Receive your yellow Animal Bite Vaccination Card detailing your scheduled return dates.
4. **Booster Doses**: Return on **Day 3**, **Day 7**, and **Day 28** for completion of the post-exposure prophylaxis protocol.
5. **14-Day Animal Observation**: Monitor the biting animal for symptoms of rabies or death over 14 days.

### 1. Registration & Assessment

Proceed to the **Animal Bite Treatment Center counter** at the Morong Rural Health Unit in Brgy. San Pedro. Bring a valid government ID or Barangay Residency Certificate.

### 2. Vaccination Schedule

The healthcare worker will administer:

- **Tetanus Toxoid** injection (to protect against tetanus infection).
- **Anti-Rabies Vaccine (ARV)**: Administered via intradermal injection on designated schedules:
  - **Day 0**: Day of initial consultation
  - **Day 3**: Second dose
  - **Day 7**: Third dose
  - **Day 28** (or Day 14/21 depending on medical protocol): Final booster dose

### 3. Animal Bite Card Reminders

- Keep your **Animal Bite Vaccination Card** safe. You must present this card on every scheduled return date.
- **Do not miss scheduled vaccine days.** Missing an appointment compromises your antibody protection and may require restarting the course.
- Avoid strenuous physical exertion and refrain from consuming alcohol during the vaccination period.
