## Standard Emergency Procedures

### Flood & Typhoon Response

1. **Preemptive Evacuation**: When MDRRMO issues Orange or Red rainfall warnings or when Morong River reaches Critical Alert levels, evacuate early while roads are passable and daylight is available.
2. **Turn Off Utilities**: Shut off main electrical breakers and gas tanks before leaving your home.
3. **Never Wade Through Moving Water**: Avoid walking or driving through floodwaters. 6 inches of moving water can knock you down, and 12 inches can sweep a vehicle away.

### Earthquake Protocol

1. **Drop, Cover, and Hold** under sturdy furniture until shaking stops.
2. **Evacuate Calmly**: Do not use elevators. Protect your head with arms or a bag while exiting to open spaces away from power lines, glass windows, and old masonry walls.
3. **Inspect for Hazards**: Check for gas leaks, electrical sparks, and structural cracks before re-entering buildings.
