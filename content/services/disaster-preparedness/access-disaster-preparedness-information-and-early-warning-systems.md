# Disaster Preparedness Information and Early Warning Systems — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipality of Morong** maintains early warning systems to alert citizens before, during, and after disasters. The system integrates national weather forecasts from DOST-PAGASA and PHIVOLCS with local river sensors, siren towers, and community radio broadcasts.

---

## Morong River Water Level Warning System

The **Morong River** and surrounding waterways are monitored 24/7 by the MDRRMO Operations Center. Local water level gauges follow color-coded alert levels:

| Alert Level                            | River Status                                 | Required Citizen Action                                                  |
| :------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Normal (Green)**                     | Water within normal riverbanks.              | Routine monitoring; stay alert to weather updates.                       |
| **Alert (Yellow)**                     | Water level approaching riverbank elevation. | Prepare family emergency Go-Bags; monitor barangay advisories.           |
| **Warning (Orange)**                   | Water overflowing minor riverbank sections.  | Voluntary / Preemptive evacuation for riverside and low-lying families.  |
| **Critical / Forced Evacuation (Red)** | Severe flooding imminent or occurring.       | Mandatory forced evacuation to designated municipal evacuation shelters. |

## Early Warning Broadcast Channels

1. **Municipal Siren & Public Address Towers**:
   - High-decibel sirens stationed in poblacion and key barangays sound distinctive horn signals during flash flood warnings or forced evacuation orders.
2. **Community Broadcasts & Mobile Megaphones**:
   - Barangay Tanods and BDRRMC teams patrol neighborhoods using vehicle-mounted sirens and public address systems.
3. **DOST-PAGASA Color-Coded Rainfall Alerts**:
   - **Yellow Warning**: Moderate to heavy rain (7.5–15 mm/hr). Flooding is possible in low-lying areas.
   - **Orange Warning**: Heavy to intense rain (15–30 mm/hr). Flooding is threatening.
   - **Red Warning**: Torrential rain (>30 mm/hr). Severe flooding expected; immediate evacuation.
4. **PHIVOLCS Earthquake & Tsunami Bulletins**:
   - Seismic activity updates along the Marikina Valley Fault System and regional earthquake occurrences.

## Emergency Kit (Go-Bag) Guidelines

Ensure your family emergency grab bag contains:

- **Water**: At least 1 gallon per person per day for at least 3 days.
- **Food**: Ready-to-eat canned goods, biscuits, energy bars (check expiration dates regularly).
- **First Aid & Medicines**: Prescription maintenance medicines, antiseptics, bandages, paracetamol, oral rehydration salts.
- **Light & Power**: LED flashlight, battery-powered transistor radio, extra batteries, fully charged power banks.
- **Sanitation**: Wet wipes, soap, alcohol, garbage bags, personal hygiene items.
- **Cash & Documents**: Cash in small denominations; waterproof ziplock bags containing IDs, birth certificates, land titles, and insurance cards.
