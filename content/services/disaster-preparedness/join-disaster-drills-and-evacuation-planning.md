# Join Disaster Drills and Evacuation Planning — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO)** of Morong, in coordination with the 8 Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Committees (BDRRMCs), the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), and DepEd, regularly organizes community disaster preparedness drills and contingency planning sessions.

---

## 1) Community & School Drills

### Nationwide Simultaneous Earthquake Drill (NSED)

- Conducted quarterly across all Morong public and private schools, commercial establishments, and municipal government buildings.
- Teaches the basic **"Duck, Cover, and Hold"** protocol, orderly evacuation to designated open holding areas (e.g., school quadrangles, town plazas, open sports fields), and headcount verification.

### Flood & Flash Flood Evacuation Drills

- Conducted specifically in low-lying riverside and coastal barangays (San Juan, San Pedro, Maybancal, Bombongan, Lagundi).
- Focuses on rapid early evacuation before floodwaters rise along the **Morong River** and Laguna de Bay.
- Familiarization with designated barangay multi-purpose evacuation centers.

---

## 2) How to Participate

1. **At the Barangay Level**:
   - Contact your **Barangay Hall** and ask the **BDRRMC** about scheduled community simulation exercises and family preparedness orientations.
2. **At School or Workplace**:
   - Cooperate with school disaster coordinators and safety marshals during unannounced and scheduled evacuation simulations.
3. **Volunteer Responders (Morong Rescue Volunteers)**:
   - Citizens interested in becoming accredited community rescue volunteers can register with MDRRMO Morong for **Basic Life Support (BLS)**, **First Aid**, and **Water Search and Rescue (WASAR)** training.

---

## 3) Family Evacuation Planning Checklist

Every household in Morong should prepare a personalized **Family Disaster Plan**:

- **Identify 2 Escape Routes**: From each room in your house and from your neighborhood to the designated barangay evacuation center.
- **Predetermined Meeting Place**: Agree on a safe meeting spot in case family members are separated during working or school hours.
- **Go-Bag (Emergency Grab Kit)**: Packed with 3 days of non-perishable food, potable drinking water, flashlight, battery radio, whistle, first aid kit, essential medicines, power bank, and waterproof copies of important family documents.
