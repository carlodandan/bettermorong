# Get a Cedula and Individual Working Permit — Morong, Rizal

This guide explains how Morong residents and workers can obtain a **Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)** from the Municipal Treasury and an **Individual Mayor's Occupational Working Permit / Health Card** for local employment.

---

## 1. Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)

The Community Tax Certificate is mandated by Republic Act No. 7160 (Local Government Code of 1991, Section 157). It serves as legal proof of residence and local tax payment, and is required for notarizing legal documents, executing affidavits, taking an oath of office, receiving salaries, applying for business licenses, and claiming government clearances.

### Who Must Secure a Cedula?

- Every resident of Morong who is eighteen (18) years of age or older and:
  - Is regularly employed on a wage or salary basis;
  - Is engaged in business or profession;
  - Owns real property with an aggregate assessed value of PhP 1,000.00 or more; or
  - Is required by law to file an income tax return.

### How Much Does a Cedula Cost?

Under national statutory rules, the Community Tax consists of:

1. **Basic Community Tax**: Fixed at **PhP 5.00** (or PhP 1.00 for individuals voluntarily paying without income).
2. **Additional Community Tax**:
   - **PhP 1.00 for every PhP 1,000.00** of gross earnings, salaries, or business receipts derived during the preceding calendar year.
   - **PhP 1.00 for every PhP 1,000.00** of assessed value of real property situated in the Philippines.
   - The statutory cap on additional tax for individuals is **PhP 5,000.00**.
3. **Late-Payment Penalty**: Community tax is due on the **first day of January** and can be paid without penalty until the **last day of February**. Payments made from March 1 onwards incur a statutory interest penalty of **2% per month** until fully paid.

### Where to Transact

- **Office**: Office of the Municipal Treasurer, Revenue Counter
- **Location**: Ground Floor, New Municipal Building, Sitio Paglabas, Morong, Rizal
- **Processing Time**: 5 to 15 minutes
- **Requirements**: Valid government-issued ID and declaration of previous year's gross income or payslip / BIR Form 2316.

## 2. Individual Mayor's Occupational Working Permit & Health Card

All private employees, salespersons, store clerks, barbers, beauticians, tricycle drivers, factory workers, and food handlers working in businesses operating within Morong must secure an annual **Individual Mayor's Working Permit** (Occupational Permit) and medical clearance.

### Overview of Steps

1. **Step 1: Medical Exam at Morong RHU** — Secure Health Certificate & laboratory tests.
2. **Step 2: Barangay Employment Clearance** — Obtain from your local Morong barangay.
3. **Step 3: Police Clearance & Cedula** — Get Police/NBI clearance and current year Cedula.
4. **Step 4: Treasury Payment & Working Permit Card** — Submit to BPLO and claim your permit ID.

### Step 1: Medical Exam & Sanitary Health Card (Municipal Health Office)

Proceed to the **Morong Rural Health Unit (RHU)** in Brgy. San Pedro for medical screening:

- **Non-Food Workers**: Physical checkup, Chest X-ray result (valid within 6 months), and Urinalysis.
- **Food Handlers & Service Workers**: Chest X-ray, Stool Examination (Fecalysis), and Hepatitis B screening.
- **Health Card Release**: Receive the official Morong Health Certificate (Pink Card for food establishments; Green/Standard Card for non-food businesses).

### Step 2: Barangay Clearance for Employment

Obtain a **Barangay Clearance for Employment** from the barangay hall of your residence or where your workplace is situated in Morong. Bring a valid ID and community tax certificate.

### Step 3: Police or NBI Clearance

Secure a **Police Clearance** from the Morong Municipal Police Station (PNP) or present a valid National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) clearance certificate.

### Step 4: Final Processing & Mayor's Permit Issuance

1. Proceed to the **Business Permits & Licensing Section (BPLS)** / Municipal Hall lobby.
2. Submit the combined dossier:
   - Accomplished [Mayor’s Working Permit Application Form (PDF)](https://morongrizal.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Working-Permit-Application-Form.pdf) (or download from our [Downloadable Forms Hub](/government/transparency-documents/downloadable-forms))
   - Original Morong RHU Health Card
   - Barangay Employment Clearance
   - Police or NBI Clearance
   - Photocopy of current year Cedula
   - 1x1 or 2x2 ID photo (2 copies)
3. Pay the official occupational permit fee at the Treasury window <!-- TODO(verify): Confirm exact fee in Morong Citizen's Charter 2025 -->.
4. Claim your official laminated **Mayor's Occupational Working Permit ID Card**, valid until December 31 of the current year.
