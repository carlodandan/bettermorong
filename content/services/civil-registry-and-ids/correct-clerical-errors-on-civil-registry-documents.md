# Correct Clerical Errors on Civil Registry Documents — Morong, Rizal

Under Philippine law, clerical and typographical errors in vital civil registry records (birth, marriage, and death certificates) registered in Morong can be corrected through administrative petitions at the **Local Civil Registry Office (LCRO)** without going through costly and prolonged court proceedings.

---

## Which Law Applies to Your Situation?

| Remedy                                      | Governing Law              | What Can Be Corrected                                                                                                       |
| :------------------------------------------ | :------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Clerical Error Petition**                 | **Republic Act No. 9048**  | Misspelled names, typographical errors, incorrect occupation, marital status of parents, or change of first name / nickname |
| **Correction of Day/Month of Birth or Sex** | **Republic Act No. 10172** | Erroneous day or month of birth (not year), or erroneous entry of biological sex/gender                                     |

---

## 1. Correction of Clerical / Typographical Errors (RA 9048)

A clerical error is a harmless typographical mistake made by writing, typing, or copying that is obvious to the eye and does not alter citizenship, legitimacy, or civil status.

### Requirements for RA 9048 (Clerical Error)

1. **Certified True Copy (CTC)** of the birth, marriage, or death certificate bearing the erroneous entry issued by both the Morong LCRO and the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA).
2. **At least two (2) public or private documents** showing the correct entry, such as:
   - Certificate of Baptism / Dedication
   - Elementary / High School Form 137 or Permanent Transcript of Records
   - GSIS, SSS, or PhilHealth Member Data Record
   - Voter Registration Record from COMELEC
   - Land Titles, Driver’s License, or valid Passport
3. **Valid Government-Issued ID** of the petitioner.
4. **Filing Fee**: Administrative filing fee paid at the Municipal Treasury <!-- TODO(verify): Confirm exact fee in Morong Citizen's Charter 2025 -->.

## 2. Change of First Name or Nickname (RA 9048)

Petitioning to change a first name requires proving specific legal grounds (e.g., the name is ridiculous, tainted with dishonor, or has been consistently and habitually used since childhood).

### Additional Requirements for Change of First Name

1. All core requirements under RA 9048 listed above.
2. **Clearance Certificates** showing the petitioner has no criminal or civil record:
   - National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) Clearance
   - Philippine National Police (PNP) Clearance
3. **Employer Certification / Certificate of Non-Employment**: Stating whether the applicant has any pending administrative liabilities.
4. **Affidavit of Publication**: Mandatory publication in a newspaper of general circulation once a week for **two (2) consecutive weeks**.

---

## 3. Correction of Day, Month of Birth, or Gender (RA 10172)

Republic Act No. 10172 allows administrative correction of erroneous entries in the **day** or **month** of birth (changing the birth _year_ still requires a judicial court order) or the **biological sex/gender** of a person where there was a clerical mistake at birth.

### Additional Strict Requirements for RA 10172

1. **Medical Certification**: For corrections of sex/gender, an official certification issued by an accredited government physician (Municipal Health Officer) certifying that the petitioner has not undergone sex reassignment surgery.
2. **Earliest School Records**: Elementary Form 137 or elementary permanent record.
3. **Baptismal Record** or equivalent religious record.
4. **NBI, Police, and Barangay Clearances** of the petitioner.
5. **Affidavit of Publication**: Mandatory publication in a newspaper of general circulation once a week for **two (2) consecutive weeks**.

---

## Petition Process & Timeline

1. **File Petition & Submit Supporting Evidence**: Submit your verified petition along with supporting records at the Morong LCRO.
2. **Legal Evaluation**: The Municipal Civil Registrar examines the documents for sufficiency and legal basis.
3. **10-Day Notice of Posting**: Notice of the petition is posted on the Municipal Hall bulletin board for ten (10) consecutive calendar days.
4. **Decision Issued**: If no opposition is filed, the Civil Registrar grants the petition and signs the official Decision.
5. **Transmittal to PSA**: The complete case dossier is transmitted to the PSA Civil Registrar General in Quezon City for affirmation.
6. **Release of Annotated PSA Certificate**: Upon PSA approval, the petitioner receives the official annotated record on security paper (SECPA).

### Processing Standard

- **Local Evaluation & Posting**: Approximately 15 to 30 calendar days (including the mandatory 10-day posting and publication review).
- **PSA Affirmation**: The Morong LCRO transmits the approved dossier to the PSA Civil Registrar General. Upon affirmation, citizens can claim their official annotated certificate on security paper through any PSA Serbilis outlet or PSA online portal.
