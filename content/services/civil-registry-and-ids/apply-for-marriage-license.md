# Apply for a Marriage License — Morong, Rizal

Couples planning to marry where either party is a resident of Morong, Rizal must secure an official **Marriage License** issued by the **Local Civil Registry Office (LCRO)** pursuant to the Family Code of the Philippines (Executive Order No. 209).

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information           | Details                                                                                          |
| :-------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where to Apply**    | Local Civil Registry Office, Ground Floor, New Municipal Building, Sitio Paglabas, Morong, Rizal |
| **Who Must Apply**    | Both contracting parties must appear in person                                                   |
| **Validity Period**   | Valid for **120 days** anywhere in the Philippines from date of issuance                         |
| **Mandatory Posting** | **10 consecutive calendar days** publication before license release                              |
| **Processing Time**   | Released on the 11th calendar day after submission and posting                                   |

---

## Step 1: Gather Documentary Requirements

Prepare all required authentic civil documents before visiting Municipal Hall:

### Core Requirements (For Both Filipino Parties)

1. **PSA Birth Certificate**: Certified True Copy issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority on security paper.
2. **PSA CENOMAR**: Certificate of No Marriage Record issued by the PSA within the last six (6) months.
3. **Barangay Certificate of Residency**: Proving at least one applicant resides in Morong, Rizal.
4. **Valid Government-Issued ID**: Photocopy and original presentation (e.g., PhilID, Passport, Driver’s License, Postal ID, Voter’s ID).
5. **Recent 2x2 Colored Photos**: Three (3) copies each against a plain white background.

### Age-Specific Statutory Requirements

- **Applicants aged 18 to 21 years old**: Mandatory **Parental Consent**. Parents (father, mother, or legal guardian) must appear in person before the Civil Registrar or execute a duly notarized Affidavit of Parental Consent.
- **Applicants aged 22 to 25 years old**: Mandatory **Parental Advice**. Parents must execute a written Advice upon Intended Marriage. If parents refuse or cannot appear, the license release will be held for three (3) months following publication.

### Special Cases

- **Widowed Applicants**: PSA Certified Copy of the Death Certificate of the deceased spouse.
- **Divorced / Annulled Applicants**: PSA Copy of Marriage Certificate with registered judicial decree of absolute nullity or annulment and Certificate of Finality from the regional trial court.
- **Foreign Citizens Marrying in Morong**:
  - Certificate of Legal Capacity to Contract Marriage issued by their embassy or consular office in the Philippines.
  - Photocopy of Passport (biographical page and date of arrival / valid visa stamp).
  - Divorce decree or death certificate of previous spouse (if applicable).

---

## Step 2: Attend Mandatory Pre-Marriage Seminars

Couples must complete two statutory seminars before the license is granted:

1. **Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counseling (PMOC)**:
   - Facilitated jointly by the **Municipal Social Welfare & Development Office (MSWDO)** and the **Municipal Health Office (MHO)**.
   - Topics include responsible parenthood, maternal and infant health, emotional readiness, and marital communication.
2. **Family Planning Seminar**:
   - Conducted by accredited healthcare providers at the Morong Rural Health Unit.
3. **Certificate of Attendance**: Secure official completion certificates from both offices and submit them to the LCRO.

---

## Step 3: File Application & 10-Day Notice of Posting

1. Both parties fill out the **Application for Marriage License (Municipal Form No. 90)** before the Civil Registrar.
2. Pay the statutory fees at the Municipal Treasurer’s Office revenue counter (**₱420.00 total** under Service #26, Morong Citizen’s Charter 2025: Application Fee ₱200, License Fee ₱50, Family Planning ₱100, PMOC ₱40, and Documentary Stamp ₱30).
3. **Mandatory 10-Day Posting**: By law, notice of the pending marriage application is posted on the public bulletin board of the Morong Municipal Hall for ten (10) consecutive calendar days to allow for any legal impediments to be reported.

---

## Step 4: License Issuance

On the **eleventh (11th) day** following successful completion of the posting period and receipt of all seminar certificates:

- Claim the signed, sealed **Marriage License (Municipal Form No. 92)** at the LCRO counter.
- Present this document to your solemnizing officer (judge, minister, pastor, or priest) prior to the wedding ceremony.
