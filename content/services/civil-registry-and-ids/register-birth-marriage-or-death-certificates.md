# Register Birth, Marriage, or Death Certificates — Morong, Rizal

The **Local Civil Registry Office (LCRO)** of Morong, Rizal processes the official recording of vital events, delayed registration filings, issuance of local certified true copies, and electronic endorsements to the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA).

---

## 1. Timely Birth Registration

Every child born within the territorial jurisdiction of the Municipality of Morong must be registered within **thirty (30) calendar days** from the date of delivery.

### What You Need to Know

| Detail                | Information                                                                                      |
| :-------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where to Transact** | Local Civil Registry Office, Ground Floor, New Municipal Building, Sitio Paglabas, Morong, Rizal |
| **Applicable Fees**   | **Free of Charge** for timely registration                                                       |
| **Processing Time**   | Same day (15 – 30 minutes upon complete documents)                                               |
| **Who Prepares Form** | Attending physician, midwife, or hospital / lying-in clinic administrative staff                 |

### Documentary Requirements

1. **Four (4) original copies of Certificate of Live Birth (COLB / Municipal Form No. 102)** signed by the attending doctor, nurse, or licensed midwife.
2. **For Married Parents**: Photocopy of PSA or Local Certificate of Marriage.
3. **For Unmarried Parents (Father acknowledging paternity)**:
   - Duly executed **Affidavit of Acknowledgment / Admission of Paternity** (found at the back of the COLB).
   - Duly notarized **Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father (AUSF)** under Republic Act No. 9255 if the child will carry the father's last name.
4. **Valid Government-Issued ID** of both parents or the informant.

## 2. Delayed (Late) Registration of Birth

If more than 30 days have elapsed since the date of birth, registration follows delayed registration procedures to verify identity and prevent double registration.

### Documentary Requirements

1. **Negative Certification of Birth** issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) stating that no record of birth exists in the national civil registry archive.
2. **Accomplished Certificate of Live Birth (COLB)** with notarized Affidavit for Delayed Registration executed at the back.
3. **Joint Affidavit of Two (2) Disinterested Persons** who have personal knowledge of the child's birth facts.
4. **Any two (2) of the following authentic supporting documents** showing name, date of birth, place of birth, and parents' names:
   - Certificate of Baptism / Dedication
   - Elementary School Form 137 / Transcript of Records
   - Voter's Registration Record / COMELEC Certification
   - PhilHealth Member Data Record (MDR)
   - Barangay Certificate of Residency of the applicant
   - Marriage Certificate (if married)
5. **Fee & Posting Period**: Timely registration within 30 days is **100% Free**; delayed registration fee is **₱230.00** (Filing fee ₱150 + Out-of-town ₱50 + Doc stamp ₱30 per Service #23, Morong Citizen’s Charter 2025) plus mandatory **ten (10) consecutive calendar days** notice of posting on the municipal bulletin board before final release.

## 3. Marriage Registration

Civil and church marriages solemnized within Morong must be filed with the Local Civil Registry:

1. **Filing Period**: Within **fifteen (15) calendar days** following the solemnization of the marriage (or within 30 days for marriages exempt from license requirement under Article 34 of the Family Code).
2. **Responsible Party**: The solemnizing officer (priest, pastor, minister, or judge) is legally responsible for transmitting the Certificate of Marriage (COM / Municipal Form No. 97).
3. **Verification**: Contracting parties may request a local Certified True Copy to confirm that their solemnizing officer properly filed their certificate.

---

## 4. Death Registration

Every death occurring within Morong must be registered promptly:

1. **Timeline**: The attending physician must certify the cause of death within forty-eight (48) hours. The nearest relative or funeral service provider must register the Certificate of Death (COD / Municipal Form No. 103) within **thirty (30) calendar days** of demise.
2. **Sanitary Inspection & Transfer**: If the remains are to be transferred out of or into Morong, secure a Transfer Permit and Sanitary Burial Permit from the Municipal Health Office before transport.
3. **Delayed Registration of Death**: Requires a Negative PSA Certification, Affidavit of Delayed Registration, and supporting records from the cemetery or church of burial.

---

## 5. Local Certified True Copies & PSA Endorsement

If you need a copy of an existing Morong civil registry document for passport applications, employment, school enrollment, or legal transactions:

### Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Fill Out Request Slip**: Inquire at the LCRO counter with complete details (full name, exact date, and place of event).
2. **Book Verification**: The registry officer searches the local registry archive book.
3. **Payment**: Pay the certified true copy fee at the Municipal Treasurer’s Office revenue window (**₱180.00** per copy with documentary stamp per Service #28, Morong Citizen’s Charter 2025; or **₱200.00** per copy if requested through the PSA BREQS Batch Request Entry System under Service #32).
4. **Document Release**: Present the Official Receipt to the LCRO releasing window for issuance of the official stamped Certified True Copy.
5. **PSA Electronic Endorsement**: If your record is missing or blurry in the national PSA database, request an official Electronic Endorsement at the Morong LCRO so local records are transmitted directly to PSA Central Office for fast-tracked security paper (SECPA) issuance.
