## 1. Transfer of Property Ownership (New Tax Declaration)

When you buy a parcel of land, inherit a property through an Extrajudicial Settlement, or receive a donation in Morong, you must transfer the Tax Declaration from the previous owner's name to your own.

### Documentary Checklist

1. **New Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT / OCT)**: Certified True Copy issued by the Registry of Deeds of Rizal (Morong Branch / Binangonan / Antipolo registry).
2. **Notarized Deed of Conveyance**: Original duplicate of the Deed of Absolute Sale, Deed of Donation, or Extrajudicial Settlement of Estate.
3. **Electronic Certificate Authorizing Registration (eCAR)**: Issued by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) Revenue District Office covering Morong, proving settlement of Capital Gains Tax / Donor’s Tax / Estate Tax and Documentary Stamp Tax.
4. **Transfer Tax Official Receipt**: Paid at the Rizal Provincial Treasury / Municipal Treasury of Morong.
5. **Real Property Tax Clearance**: Issued by the Morong Municipal Treasurer certifying that taxes for the current year are fully paid.
6. **Previous Tax Declaration**: Photocopy of the canceled Tax Declaration in the name of the previous registered owner.
7. **Barangay Certification**: For untitled alienable and disposable agricultural lands under ancestral possession.

### Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Document Presentation**: Present your Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT), notarized deed, and BIR eCAR at the Assessor's Assessment Desk.
2. **Cadastral Map Verification**: Assessor staff cross-references the property index, tax mapping, and cadastral lot number.
3. **Field Verification**: An ocular inspection is conducted if newly constructed improvements or building expansions are involved.
4. **Approval & Cancellation**: The Municipal Assessor approves the assessment and cancels the previous owner's Tax Declaration.
5. **Issuance of New TD**: Claim your official Notice of Assessment and new Tax Declaration in the current owner's name.

## 2. Declaration of Newly Constructed Buildings or Improvements

If you have constructed a house, commercial building, warehouse, or perimeter fence, you are legally required to declare the new improvement to the Municipal Assessor within sixty (60) days of completion or occupancy:

### Requirements

1. Photocopy of the **Approved Building Permit** and **Certificate of Occupancy** issued by the Office of the Municipal Engineer.
2. Approved building plans / architectural specifications indicating total floor area and construction cost.
3. Current Tax Declaration and Title of the parcel of land where the building was erected.
4. Ocular inspection report prepared by the Municipal Assessor's field appraisal team.

## 3. Requesting Assessor Certifications

The Municipal Assessor issues specialized certifications often requested for court proceedings, bank loans, scholarships, and building permit clearances:

### 1. Certified True Copy (CTC) / Computer Printout of Tax Declaration

- **Official Fee:** **₱180.00** per Tax Declaration (including Documentary Stamp Tax).
- **Processing Time:** **17 minutes** (Service #2, Morong Citizen’s Charter 2025).
- **Requirements:** Accomplished Request Form, latest Real Property Tax Receipt, valid ID of registered owner, and authorization letter if represented.

### 2. Certificate of Property Holdings / Non-Holding

- **Official Fee:** **₱180.00** with documentary stamp.
- **Processing Time:** **30 minutes** (Service #3, Morong Citizen’s Charter 2025).
- **Purpose:** Certifies all parcels of real property registered in an individual’s name, or certifies that the applicant owns zero real estate in Morong (commonly required for indigent medical aid, scholarship applications, and socialized housing).
- **Requirements:** Letter request, valid ID, and Barangay Certificate of Residency.

### 3. Certificate of No Improvement

- **Official Fee:** **₱180.00** with documentary stamp.
- **Processing Time:** **30 minutes** (Service #3, Morong Citizen’s Charter 2025).
- **Purpose:** Certifies that a specific titled lot is vacant and has no existing residential or commercial structure erected upon it.
- **Requirements:** TCT copy, recent dated photos of the vacant lot, and inspection confirmation.
