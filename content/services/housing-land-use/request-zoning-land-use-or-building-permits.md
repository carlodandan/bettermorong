# Zoning Clearances, Land Use & Building Permits — Morong, Rizal

Before initiating any residential, commercial, industrial, or institutional construction, expansion, or land development in the Municipality of Morong, property owners and developers must secure a **Locational / Zoning Clearance** and a **Building Permit** in accordance with the Morong Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP), Zoning Ordinance, and the National Building Code of the Philippines (PD 1096).

---

## 1. Locational / Zoning Clearance (MPDO)

The Locational Clearance confirms that the proposed building or business activity conforms to the designated land-use classification (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Agricultural, or Eco-Tourism).

- **Official Form**: [Locational Clearance Application Form (PDF)](https://morongrizal.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/APPLICATION-FORM-FOR-LOCATIONAL-CLEARANCE.pdf)

### Requirements:

1. Certified True Copy of **Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT)** or Deed of Absolute Sale.
2. Current **Tax Declaration** from the Municipal Assessor's Office and Real Property Tax (Amilyar) Official Receipt.
3. Barangay Clearance for Construction / Development.
4. Five (5) sets of Complete Architectural / Engineering Building Plans signed and sealed by licensed professionals.
5. Bill of Materials and Cost Estimates.
6. Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) or Certificate of Non-Coverage (CNC) from DENR (for commercial, poultry, or industrial projects).

---

## 2. Building Permit (Office of the Building Official / MEO)

Issued by the **Municipal Engineering Office / Building Official** authorizing physical construction works.

### Required Permit Documents:

- **Official Forms & Checklists**:
  - [Revised Building Permit Application Form (PDF)](https://morongrizal.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Building-Permit-Revised-Application-Form.pdf)
  - [Building Permit Requirements Checklist (PDF)](https://morongrizal.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Building-Permit-Requirements-New-or-Renovation.pdf)
  - [Electrical Permit Application Form (PDF)](https://morongrizal.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Electrical-Permit-Application-Form.pdf)
  - [Application for Certificate of Occupancy (PDF)](https://morongrizal.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Application-Form-for-Certificate-of-Occupancy.pdf)
  - _For demolition, excavation, fencing, or sign permits, access the complete directory on our [Downloadable Forms Hub](/government/transparency-documents/downloadable-forms)._
- Complete sets of Plans:
  - Architectural Plans (sealed by a Registered Architect)
  - Civil / Structural Plans with Structural Analysis for buildings 2 storeys and above (sealed by a Civil Engineer)
  - Sanitary / Plumbing Plans (sealed by a Sanitary Engineer or Master Plumber)
  - Electrical Plans (sealed by a Professional Electrical Engineer)
  - Mechanical Plans (if installing elevators, central air, or industrial machinery)
- Soil Investigation Report (for commercial, multi-storey, or bridge structures).
- Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) issued by the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) Morong Fire Station.

---

## Processing Steps & Timelines

1. **Submit Application to the One-Stop Shop for Construction Permits (OSCP)**:
   - Location: Ground Floor, New Municipal Building, Sitio Paglabas, San Pedro, Morong.
2. **Joint Technical Evaluation**:
   - The Zoning Administrator (MPDO), Building Official (MEO), and BFP conduct a coordinated assessment of plans.
3. **Assessment & Payment**:
   - Pay official regulatory fees at the Municipal Treasurer's Cashier.
4. **Issuance of Permit**:
   - Simple residential permits processed within **3 to 5 business days** pursuant to Republic Act No. 11032 (Ease of Doing Business Act).
