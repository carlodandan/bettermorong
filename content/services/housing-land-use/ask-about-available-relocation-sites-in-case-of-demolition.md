# Relocation Sites & Displacement Safeguards — Morong, Rizal

In cases where public infrastructure expansion, riverbank dredging, or court-ordered clearing operations affect informal settlers or residential structures, the **Municipality of Morong** adheres strictly to the statutory safeguards of the **Urban Development and Housing Act (Republic Act No. 7279)**.

---

## Statutory Protections Under RA 7279

The local government ensures that no arbitrary or unannounced evictions or demolitions occur within Morong:

1. **Mandatory 30-Day Notice**: Formal written notice served to affected occupants at least thirty (30) days prior to any planned clearing or relocation.
2. **Adequate Consultations**: Public dialogue conducted jointly by the LGU, MSWDO, the Presidential Commission for the Urban Poor (PCUP), and affected residents.
3. **Presence of LGU & Human Rights Observers**: Eviction or clearing activities must be witnessed by local officials, social workers, and human rights representatives during daytime and fair weather only.
4. **Relocation or Financial Assistance**: Affected underprivileged families are entitled to safe, habitable relocation options or financial compensation to avoid homelessness.

---

## Relocation Sites & Safe Resettlement

- **Government-Assisted Resettlement Areas**: Relocation sites coordinated with the National Housing Authority (NHA) and provincial housing agencies in designated development zones equipped with potable water, electricity, road access, and educational facilities.
- **Temporary Transition Centers**: For families requiring immediate shelter following structural eviction or riverbank danger clearing, temporary accommodation is provided through municipal evacuation and transitional housing facilities in coordination with MSWDO and MDRRMO.
