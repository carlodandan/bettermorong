# Socialized Housing & Relocation Assistance — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipality of Morong**, through the **Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO)** and the **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)**, coordinates with the Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD) and National Housing Authority (NHA) to provide shelter assistance, socialized housing coordination, and disaster relocation for low-income and danger-zone informal settler families.

---

## Housing Assistance Programs

1. **4PH (Pambansang Pabahay Para sa Pilipino) Program Coordination**:
   - Facilitating community profiling and pre-qualification for national socialized housing and mid-rise residential community projects.
2. **Disaster Recovery Shelter Support (LGSF-DRRAP & NHA)**:
   - Distribution of emergency roofing and shelter construction materials for low-income families whose homes were damaged or destroyed by natural calamities and typhoons.
3. **Danger-Zone Resettlement**:
   - Planned relocation for informal settler families residing along high-risk floodways, waterways (Morong River, Calero Creek, and Talaga Creek embankments), and landslide-prone mountain slopes in accordance with Republic Act No. 7279 (Urban Development and Housing Act).

---

## Eligibility Criteria

- Bonafide resident of Morong, Rizal, verified by the Barangay and MSWDO social census.
- Does not own any existing titled residential property or real estate in any part of the Philippines.
- Family monthly income within the statutory low-income / socialized housing threshold.
- Verified occupant of an identified danger zone or displaced by a disaster.

---

## Application Requirements

1. Barangay Certificate of Indigency and Residency (minimum 3 years residency).
2. PSA Marriage Certificate (if married) and PSA Birth Certificates of family members.
3. Valid Government-issued IDs of spouses or household head.
4. Proof of household income (Certificate of Compensation, ITR, or social worker assessment).
5. Formal intake interview and Social Case Study Report conducted by the MSWDO.
