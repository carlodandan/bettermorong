# Pay Real Property Tax (Amilyar) and Get Tax Clearance — Morong, Rizal

Property owners in the **Municipality of Morong, Province of Rizal** are required by Republic Act No. 7160 (Local Government Code of 1991) to pay annual **Real Property Tax (RPT / Amilyar)** on all taxable lands, residential and commercial buildings, and machinery located within the municipality.

## 1. Real Property Tax Structure

The annual Real Property Tax levied on real properties in Morong comprises two statutory components based on the property's **Assessed Value** (as indicated on your official Tax Declaration issued by the Municipal Assessor):

1. **Basic Real Property Tax**: 1% of the assessed property value (provincial standard rate).
2. **Special Education Fund (SEF)**: Additional 1% of the assessed property value, dedicated to supporting local public school facilities, books, and educational infrastructure in Morong.
3. **Total Annual Rate**: **2% of the Assessed Value** (Basic RPT + SEF).

> [!NOTE]
> Tax is computed on the property’s **Assessed Value**, not its market appraisal value. Assessed Value is determined by multiplying the fair market value by the assessment level prescribed for the property class (e.g., residential, agricultural, or commercial).

## 2. Deadlines and Prompt Payment Discounts

Property owners may pay in full or divide their tax obligation into four equal quarterly installments:

| Installment     | Quarter Period     | Payment Deadline |
| :-------------- | :----------------- | :--------------- |
| **1st Quarter** | January – March    | **March 31**     |
| **2nd Quarter** | April – June       | **June 30**      |
| **3rd Quarter** | July – September   | **September 30** |
| **4th Quarter** | October – December | **December 31**  |

### Early-Payment Discounts & Penalties

- **Advance Payment Discount**: Property owners who settle their full annual real property tax before January 1 or within the early-bird prompt payment period in January enjoy statutory promptness discounts (up to 10% to 20% discount depending on the prevailing municipal tax ordinance) <!-- TODO(verify): Confirm exact promptness discount percentage in Morong Citizen's Charter 2025 -->.
- **Late-Payment Penalty**: Unpaid taxes after each quarterly deadline incur a statutory surcharge and interest penalty of **2% per month** on the unpaid amount until fully paid.

## 3. How to Pay at the Municipal Hall

### Requirements to Bring

1. **Previous Year’s Official Receipt (O.R.)** of Real Property Tax payment; **OR**
2. **Photocopy of the latest Tax Declaration** (issued by the Morong Municipal Assessor's Office).
3. If transacting through a representative: Authorization Letter and valid government-issued ID of both the owner and the representative.

### Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Assessment Verification**: Present your previous receipt or Tax Declaration number at Window 1 / Assessment Desk of the Municipal Treasurer’s Office. The teller will verify the ledger card and compute the current year’s bill, applying any early-bird discounts or arrears.
2. **Payment**: Pay the assessed amount in cash or cashier's check payable to the _Municipal Treasurer of Morong_.
3. **Official Receipt (O.R.)**: Receive your official stamped electronic Official Receipt. Keep this receipt as proof of payment and bring it for your next renewal.

---

## 4. How to Request a Real Property Tax Clearance

A **Real Property Tax Clearance** (Certificate of No Tax Delinquency) is required when selling property, transferring ownership at the Registry of Deeds, applying for building permits, bank mortgages, or titling transactions.

### Requirements for Clearance

1. Fully settled Real Property Tax for the **current calendar year** (Official Receipt presentation).
2. Photocopy of latest **Tax Declaration** for both land and improvements/building.
3. Valid government-issued ID of the property owner or authorized representative.
4. Payment of the nominal Tax Clearance Certificate fee <!-- TODO(verify): Confirm exact fee in Morong Citizen's Charter 2025 -->.

### Release

The Municipal Treasurer issues and signs the Tax Clearance Certificate, certifying that the subject parcel of land or structure has zero tax arrears or liens.
