# Participate in Local Trade Fairs, Business Expos, and Tourism Promotion Activities — Morong, Rizal

Showcase your products and services, connect with institutional buyers, and promote your brand by participating in municipal, provincial, and regional trade expos organized in the **Municipality of Morong, Province of Rizal**.

---

## Who Can Participate

- **Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)**: Local producers, food processors, bakers, garment makers, and craftsmen based in Morong.
- **Agricultural & Fishery Producers**: Local farmers’ cooperatives, organic growers, and fisherfolk associations from Laguna de Bay lakeshore communities.
- **Tourism & Hospitality Enterprises**: Farm resorts, eco-parks, restaurants, cafes, event coordinators, and recreational operators.
- **Creative Artisans & Cultural Groups**: Traditional artisans, painters, woodcraft makers, and youth entrepreneurs.

---

## Signature Municipal Events & Bazaars

1. **Sanrok sa Ringring Festival Trade Fair** (December):
   - Annual town cultural celebration featuring culinary showcases, local delicacy bazaars, and MSME exhibits at the Morong Town Plaza.
2. **Feast of St. Jerome Town Fiesta Bazaars** (First weekend of February / September):
   - High-foot-traffic agro-industrial trade fair featuring fresh produce, native delicacies, handicrafts, and commercial merchandise.
3. **One Town, One Product (OTOP) Hubs & Regional Fairs**:
   - DTI-assisted exhibits at SM City Taytay, Ynares Center Antipolo, and Manila trade expositions.

---

## How to Register for an Event

1. **Prepare Business Eligibility Documents**:
   - Valid DTI / SEC Registration.
   - Current Year Morong Mayor’s Business Permit.
   - Barangay Business Clearance.
   - Food and Drug Administration (FDA) CPR / LTO (for packaged food and cosmetics).
2. **Coordinate with the Morong Tourism Office or DTI Negosyo Center**:
   - Submit a formal Letter of Intent or accomplish the Exhibitor Registration Form detailing your product lineup and space requirements.
3. **Booth Allocation & Pre-Event Briefing**:
   - Attend the exhibitors' orientation covering stall assignments, ingress/egress schedules, electrical guidelines, and waste segregation rules.
4. **Ingress & Sales Operations**:
   - Set up your display booths at the Morong Town Plaza or designated event grounds, ensuring clear price tags and cashless payment options (e.g. GCash/Maya QR codes).
