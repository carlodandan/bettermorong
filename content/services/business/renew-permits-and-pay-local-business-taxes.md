# Renew Permits and Pay Local Business Taxes — Morong, Rizal

All registered enterprises operating within the **Municipality of Morong, Province of Rizal** must renew their **Mayor’s Business Permit** and settle their **Local Business Taxes (LBT)** annually during the **Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS)** period in January.

---

## Who Must Renew

- All businesses maintaining an office, store, stall, workshop, manufacturing plant, or commercial enterprise in Morong.
- Covers sole proprietorships, partnerships, corporations, cooperatives, clinics, financial institutions, and public market stallholders.

---

## Deadlines & Payment Options

- **Annual Renewal Window**: **January 1 to January 20** of every calendar year (unless formally extended by Sangguniang Bayan resolution).
- **Payment Modes**:
  - **Full Annual Payment**: Pay total assessment in full by January 20.
  - **Quarterly Installments**:
    - 1st Quarter: On or before January 20
    - 2nd Quarter: On or before April 20
    - 3rd Quarter: On or before July 20
    - 4th Quarter: On or before October 20
- **Penalties for Late Payment**: A 25% surcharge plus a 2% monthly interest on unpaid taxes are assessed pursuant to the Local Government Code and Morong Municipal Revenue Code.

---

## Required Renewal Documents

1. **Prior Year Documents**:
   - Previous Year’s Mayor’s Business Permit and Official Receipt.
   - Previous Year’s Barangay Business Clearance.
2. **Gross Sales / Receipts Basis**:
   - Sworn Statement of Gross Sales / Receipts for the preceding calendar year.
   - VAT Returns (BIR Form 2550M/Q) or Percentage Tax Returns (BIR Form 2551Q) or Audited Financial Statements with BIR stamp.
3. **Current Clearances**:
   - New **Barangay Business Clearance** for the current calendar year.
   - **Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)** for the company and owner from the Municipal Treasurer’s Office.
   - Comprehensive General Liability Insurance (CGLI), if applicable to the business type.

---

## The Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS) Flow

During January, the municipal government convenes the Business One-Stop Shop at the Morong Municipal Hall:

1. **Step 1: Document Submission & Verification**
   - Present your renewal application and gross sales declaration at the BPLS receiving window.
2. **Step 2: Joint Assessment of Taxes and Fees**
   - The Municipal Treasurer's Office computes Local Business Taxes, garbage fees (Ordinance No. 5, S. 2014), sanitary inspection fees, and regulatory charges.
3. **Step 3: Integrated Clearances Review**
   - Simultaneous verification by the Municipal Health Office (Sanitary Permit), MENRO (Environmental Clearance), MPDO (Zoning), and Bureau of Fire Protection (FSIC assessment).
4. **Step 4: Payment at Treasury**
   - Settle computed fees at the Municipal Treasurer cashier window and secure your official receipt.
5. **Step 5: Release of Mayor’s Permit Plate & Sticker**
   - Claim your official Mayor’s Business Permit certificate, updated business plate sticker, and sanitary permit.
