# Public Facilities, Gyms & Multipurpose Centers — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipality of Morong** maintains public facilities, multipurpose covered courts, civic grounds, and community halls across the municipality for sports, civic events, youth assemblies, cultural presentations, and private gatherings.

---

## Key Municipal & Barangay Facilities

1. **Morong Municipal Gymnasium / Sports Center**: Located within the civic center area in San Pedro; ideal for town-wide sports leagues, civic assemblies, and large community functions.
2. **Morong Town Plaza (Morong Park)**: Historic public plaza in Poblacion, San Pedro, suited for open-air cultural celebrations, flag ceremonies, and community gatherings.
3. **Barangay Multipurpose Covered Courts**:
   - **San Jose**: Two covered courts (Barangay Proper and Upper Balante)
   - **Bombongan**: Covered court and sports grounds
   - **San Pedro**: Plaza and community activity center
   - **Lagundi, Maybancal, CCL, San Guillermo, San Juan**: Barangay-maintained covered courts
4. **Barangay Assembly Halls**: Meeting rooms and conference halls for accredited civic groups, senior citizens, and Sangguniang Kabataan assemblies.

---

## Guidelines for Facility Use & Reservation

### Priority Classifications:

1. **LGU & Official Government Events**: Disaster response, public health vaccination missions, and official town functions hold automatic priority.
2. **Civic & Youth Activities**: Sangguniang Kabataan leagues, school competitions, and NGO community services.
3. **Private Gatherings**: Weddings, birthdays, and sports club games (subject to schedule availability and maintenance utility fee).

### How to Reserve a Facility:

1. **Submit a Letter of Request**:
   - For the **Municipal Gym / Town Plaza**: Address request to the **Office of the Municipal Mayor**, Morong Municipal Hall.
   - For **Barangay Covered Courts & Halls**: Address request to the **Punong Barangay** at the respective Barangay Hall.
2. **Specify Details**: Event date, time, expected number of participants, and requested electrical/sound equipment.
3. **Secure Permit & Settle Fees**: Pay applicable cleaning/utility fee (if required for private non-civic events) at the Municipal Treasurer's Office or Barangay Cashier.
4. **Observe Civic Rules**: All users must adhere to clean-as-you-go (CLAYGO) guidelines and Morong municipal anti-smoking and noise ordinances.
