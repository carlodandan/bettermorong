# Access to Barangay Water Supply Systems — Morong, Rizal

Clean and reliable potable water is vital for all Morongueños. The **Municipality of Morong**, together with local Barangay Water and Sanitation Associations (BAWASA) and accredited water service providers, facilitates household and community water connections across urban and rural zones.

---

## Water Supply Structure in Morong

1. **Barangay Level Water Systems (BAWASA & LGU Wells)**: Rural and upland barangays (including Lagundi, Bombongan, and San Guillermo) utilize Level II and Level III groundwater pumping stations and communal storage reservoirs funded under Assistance to Municipalities (AM) and local capital outlays.
2. **Commercial / Piped Water Utilities**: Urban poblacion areas (San Pedro, San Juan, San Jose, Maybancal, CCL) are connected through authorized private and concessionaire water pipelines.

---

## Applying for a Barangay Water Connection

### Requirements:

1. **Barangay Clearance**: Stating proof of residency and property occupancy.
2. **Proof of Ownership / Tenancy**: Land title, tax declaration, or written authorization from the property owner.
3. **Location Sketch / Vicinity Map**: Showing distance from the nearest existing water distribution main.
4. **Valid Government ID**: Voter's ID, National ID, or PhilHealth ID.

### Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. **Secure Barangay Endorsement**: Visit your Barangay Hall to verify whether your residence falls under a BAWASA system or private utility line.
2. **Technical Feasibility Assessment**: The water utility technician or barangay plumber conducts an on-site tapping inspection to estimate pipe distance and pressure.
3. **Payment of Tapping & Meter Fees**: Pay official connection and meter installation fees at the authorized waterworks office or barangay cashier.
4. **Meter Installation & Activation**: The connection is installed and water service activated within 3 to 5 business days upon permit approval.

## Inquiries & Assistance

- **Municipal Planning & Development Office (MPDO)**: 2nd Floor, Morong Municipal Hall, Sitio Paglabas, San Pedro, Morong.
- **Barangay Offices**: For BAWASA communities in Lagundi, Bombongan, and San Guillermo, coordinate directly with your respective Barangay Council on Infrastructure.
