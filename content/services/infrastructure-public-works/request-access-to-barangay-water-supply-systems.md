# Access to Water Supply Systems & Utility Connections — Morong, Rizal

Clean and reliable potable water is vital for all Morongueños. Water service in the **Municipality of Morong** is provided through two distinct systems: the **Morong Water District (MOWAD)**, which serves the poblacion and major commercial/residential corridors, and **Barangay Water and Sanitation Associations (BAWASA / BRWASA)**, which operate community-managed deep wells and reservoirs in rural and upland sitios.

## 1. Applying for a Morong Water District (MOWAD) Connection

Residents and business establishments in MOWAD-franchised areas apply for individual metered water connections directly with MOWAD:

### Documentary Requirements

1. **Proof of Property Ownership or Tenancy**:
   - Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT), Tax Declaration, or Notarized Deed of Absolute Sale.
   - For tenants or informal occupants: Notarized Letter of Consent / Authorization from the registered landowner, accompanied by the owner's valid government ID.
2. **Barangay Clearance for Water Connection**: Issued by your local Morong barangay hall.
3. **Valid Government-Issued ID**: Original presentation and photocopy (PhilID, Driver's License, Passport, Voter's ID).
4. **Excavation Permit**: If the service lateral line will traverse a municipal or provincial road, secure an excavation permit from the Office of the Municipal Engineer.

### Application Steps

1. **Filing of Application**: Visit the MOWAD Main Office on T. Claudio St., Sitio Paglabas, San Pedro, Morong. Fill out the New Service Application Form.
2. **On-Site Technical Feasibility Inspection**: A MOWAD technical inspector visits your property to inspect the nearest distribution main, water pressure, pipe distance, and materials needed.
3. **Pre-Membership Seminar**: Attend the brief new water concessionaire orientation regarding water conservation and billing rules.
4. **Settlement of Connection & Meter Fees**: Pay the new service connection, water meter deposit, and inspection fees at the MOWAD cashier <!-- TODO(verify): Confirm exact MOWAD new connection fee matrix -->.
5. **Installation**: MOWAD plumbers install the tapping valve, lateral pipe, and sealed water meter within 3 to 7 working days.

## 2. Barangay Water Systems (BAWASA / LGU Wells)

In upland or interior sitio areas not reached by MOWAD pipelines, water is sourced from municipal and barangay Level II (communal faucets) or Level III (piped household) pumping stations funded through the Local Government Support Fund (LGSF) and municipal infrastructure outlays:

### How to Connect

1. **Inquire at Barangay Hall**: Coordinate with the Barangay Committee on Infrastructure (in Bombongan, Lagundi, or San Guillermo).
2. **Barangay Endorsement**: Verify pipeline capacity and secure approval from the local BAWASA board.
3. **Tapping & Sub-metering**: Pay the community connection fee and install an approved water sub-meter.
4. **Maintenance & Monthly Dues**: Settle regular monthly communal maintenance and electricity pump dues with the designated barangay water treasurer.

---

## 3. Reporting Water Leaks, Low Pressure, or Outages

Unattended pipe leaks lead to non-revenue water loss, road damage, and contamination:

- **For MOWAD Pipeline Leaks**: Call the MOWAD Emergency Service Crew at **(02) 8691-5822**, **(02) 8691-5645**, or **(02) 8470-0922**. State the exact landmark, barangay, and street name.
- **For Barangay Pump Outages / Dirty Water**: Immediately report to your Barangay Hall or the Municipal Engineering Office at **(02) 7212-5741**.
