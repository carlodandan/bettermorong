# Report Damaged Roads, Bridges, or Drainage — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Engineering Office (MEO)** is responsible for maintaining municipal road networks, pedestrian walkways, public bridges, and stormwater drainage systems across Morong's 8 barangays. Citizens can submit maintenance and repair requests directly to the LGU.

---

## Infrastructure Under Municipal Jurisdiction

1. **Municipal & Barangay Roads**: Internal local thoroughfares, alleyways, and farm-to-market roads (e.g. Venancio St., Daang Hari in Bombongan; Sitio Labas in San Guillermo; Calero Creek road in CCL).
   > _Note: Manila East Road is a National Highway under DPWH Rizal 2nd District Engineering Office; the MEO coordinates national highway maintenance directly with DPWH._
2. **Bridges & Culverts**: Bridges crossing the Morong River, Calero Creek, and Talaga Creek.
3. **Stormwater Drainage & Canals**: Curb-and-gutter drainage, box culverts, and open canals subject to seasonal monsoon overflow.
4. **Street Lighting**: Solar streetlights and public municipal lighting poles along local streets.

---

## How to Report Damaged Infrastructure

1. **Option A: Online or Phone Reporting**
   - Call the **Municipal Engineering Office**: `(02) 8786-9398`
   - In case of road collapse or imminent flood hazard, notify the **24/7 MDRRMO Hotline**: `0917-135-0541` / `(02) 7212-5741`
2. **Option B: Barangay Incident Blotter / Endorsement**
   - File a maintenance request with your local Barangay Hall.
   - The Punong Barangay endorses the request to the Municipal Engineering Office for site inspection.
3. **Option C: In-Person Service Request**
   - Visit the **Municipal Engineering Office**, Ground Floor, New Municipal Building, Sitio Paglabas, San Pedro, Morong.
   - Provide the exact street name, barangay, landmark, and photo if available.

---

## Repair & Assessment Timeline

- **Emergency Hazards (Open manholes, fallen road debris, bridge fissures)**: Initial response and barrier placement within **24 hours**.
- **Routine Pothole Patching & Drainage De-clogging**: Site inspection within 3 business days; scheduled for crew dispatch within standard maintenance cycles.
