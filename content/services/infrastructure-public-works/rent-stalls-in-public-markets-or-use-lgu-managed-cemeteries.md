# Rent Stalls in Public Markets & Cemetery Services — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipality of Morong** manages the **Morong Public Market** and municipal cemetery grounds, providing affordable commercial stall leases for local vendors and dignified public cemetery burial services for Morong residents.

---

## 1. Morong Public Market Stall Leasing

The **Morong Public Market**, situated in Barangay San Pedro, is the town's premier trading venue for fresh produce, meats, dry goods, and local delicacies. The facility features a modern **Vegetable and Fruit Section** constructed with PhP 9,000,000.00 funding under the national Local Government Support Fund (LGSF-FALGU).

### Available Stall Classifications:

- **Wet Section**: Fresh fish from Laguna de Bay, sea fish, pork, beef, and dressed chicken.
- **Vegetable & Fruit Section**: Dedicated fresh produce trading stalls funded under LGSF-FALGU.
- **Dry Goods & Grocery Section**: Rice, grains, packaged food, condiments, and household wares.
- **Carinderia & Native Delicacies Section**: Prepared meals, snacks, and traditional Morong products.

### How to Apply for a Market Stall:

1. **Check Stall Vacancy**: Inquire with the **Market Administrator / Municipal Treasurer's Office** at the Morong Municipal Hall.
2. **Submit Application Documents**:
   - Filled-out Market Stall Lease Application Form.
   - Barangay Clearance and Proof of Residency in Morong (Morongueño vendors are given statutory priority).
   - Valid Government-issued ID and recent 2x2 photo.
   - Police or NBI Clearance.
3. **Evaluation & Awarding**: The Market Committee reviews applications based on product type and vendor qualifications.
4. **Lease Contract & Mayor's Permit**: Execute the official Contract of Lease, pay the monthly market rental fee at the Municipal Treasury, and secure a Mayor's Business Permit and Sanitary Permit.

## 2. Municipal Cemetery Services

The local government oversees the **Morong Municipal Cemetery** to guarantee orderly, hygienic, and affordable burial spaces for deceased constituents.

### Services Available:

- Application for Municipal Ground or Apartment-Type Burial Niches.
- Renewal of 5-year burial permits.
- Exhumation and transfer of skeletal remains permits.
- Free indigent burial assistance in coordination with the Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO).

### Requirements for Burial Permit:

1. Certified True Copy of the **Death Certificate** registered with the Local Civil Registry Office (LCRO).
2. Transfer Permit (if remains originate from outside Morong).
3. Barangay Indigency Certificate (for indigent burial fee exemptions).
4. Official Receipt of Burial Fees issued by the Municipal Treasurer's Office.
