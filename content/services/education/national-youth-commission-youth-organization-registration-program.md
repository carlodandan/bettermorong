# National Youth Commission’s Youth Organization Registration Program (YORP) — Morong, Rizal

All Youth and Youth-Serving Organizations in the Municipality of Morong may register with the **Morong Youth Development Office (MYDO)** and be assisted with their registration to the **National Youth Commission’s Youth Organization Registration Program (NYC YORP)**.

---

## 1) Overview

The Youth Organization Registration Program (YORP) officially registers and recognizes youth and youth-serving organizations to empower them to participate in local youth development councils, access government youth development programs, and collaborate on municipal initiatives.

---

## 2) General Requirements

All applying organizations must submit the following core documents:

1. **Accomplished Youth Organization Profile Form** _(forms can be downloaded at [https://yorpnyc.org.ph/downloadables/](https://yorpnyc.org.ph/downloadables/))_
2. **Directory of Officers and Adviser** — Both hard copy and soft copy (in Excel format `.xlsx`)
3. **List of Members in Good Standing** — Both hard copy and soft copy (in Excel format `.xlsx`)
4. **Copy of Constitution and By-Laws**
5. **Endorsement / Certification from the appropriate authority**

---

## 3) Additional Requirements by Organization Type

Depending on your organization's classification, submit the corresponding documents:

### A. Community-Based Organizations

_(Submit one of the following criteria):_

- **If the organization has a physical office:**
  - Certification of the existence of the office (specifying where the office is located).
- **If the organization does NOT have a physical office:**
  - Barangay Certification of Residence of the President.
  - Resolution of endorsement from the local Sangguniang Kabataan (SK).

### B. School-Based Organizations

- Certification of registration or official school recognition.

### C. Faith-Based Organizations

- Certification of registration or recognition from the religious denomination or governing body.

### D. Chapter of Multi-Level Organizations

- Certification of registration or official charter recognition from the national/regional parent body.

### E. Consortium Organizations

- Certification of member organizations comprising the consortium.

---

## 4) Step-by-Step Registration Procedure

Organizations can choose between on-site submission or digital/online application:

### Option A: On-site Application

1. **Secure Forms:** Obtain and fill out the forms and checklist of requirements from the Morong Youth Development Office.
2. **Submit Documents:** Submit the complete documentary requirements at the office.
3. **Verification & Scheduling:** Wait for verification and notice of the certificate issuance schedule.
4. **Claim Certificate:** Receive the official Certificate of Registration.
5. **Logbook:** Sign the office logbook.

_Processing Time:_ **4 days, 40 minutes**

### Option B: Online Application

1. **Prepare Scanned Files:** Prepare complete digital copies of all required forms and spreadsheets.
2. **Email Submission:** Send requirements via email to:
   - **`steventantiongcodeleon@gmail.com`**
3. **Issuance:** Receive the official Certification of Registration via your registered email address.

_Processing Time:_ **6 days, 40 minutes**
