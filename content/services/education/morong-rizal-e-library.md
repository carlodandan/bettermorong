# Morong, Rizal e-Library — Public Digital Learning & Research Center

> **Theme:** _"Morong E-Library, Connecting Minds, Empowering Futures"_

The **Morong, Rizal e-Library** is a free public digital learning and community research facility established by the Municipal Government of Morong. Situated along Gov. Tupas Street in Barangay San Pedro, the e-Library provides students, researchers, educators, jobseekers, and residents with free access to desktop computer workstations, printing and photocopying services, and multimedia collaboration displays.

---

## Project Background & Funding Source

### English Translation

The e-Library is a project of the Municipal Government of Morong, funded through the incentive award received from the municipality's conferment of the **Seal of Good Local Governance (SGLG)** by the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG).

Furthermore, all computer units are installed with **STARBOOKS** (_Science and Technology Academic-Research-Based Openly-Operated KioskS_) from the **Department of Science and Technology (DOST)**, providing supplemental access to scientific data, educational resources, and academic literature for learning and investigative research.

## Available Equipment & Hardware Amenities

The e-Library is fully equipped with modern digital learning hardware to support academic excellence and digital literacy:

- **Nine (9) Desktop Computers**: High-speed internet-connected workstations pre-installed with **DOST STARBOOKS**, equipped for academic research, school assignments, digital learning, thesis encoding, and civil service review.
- **Two (2) Multi-Function Printers**: Free/subsidized document printing for school outputs, project requirements, and resumes.
- **One (1) Photocopy Machine**: Heavy-duty document reproduction for study modules and review sheets.
- **Two (2) 45” Smart TVs**: Digital instructional screens for small group study sessions, online seminars, and educational video presentations.
- **One (1) 65” Smart TV**: Large-format interactive display for community learning workshops, webinars, and civic lectures.
- **One (1) 21” Full HD (FHD) Monitor**: Specialized display monitor for administrative coordination and reference lookup.

## DOST STARBOOKS Digital Science Library

All 9 desktop computer units are pre-installed with **STARBOOKS** (_Science and Technology Academic-Research-Based Openly-Operated KioskS_), developed by the **Department of Science and Technology (DOST)**:

- **Offline Digital Library ("Library-in-a-Box")**: Provides learners and researchers with access to thousands of digitized science, technology, mathematics, and engineering resources that operate seamlessly both online and offline.
- **Academic Research Materials**: Contains full-text scientific journals, Philippine science heritage archives, investigatory project (IP) blueprints, encyclopedias, and K-12 interactive STEM learning modules.
- **Multimedia Educational Lessons**: Features video lessons on disaster preparedness, agricultural technologies, health science, and local innovations.

## User Guidelines & House Rules

1. **Registration & Entry**:
   - Visitors must present a valid Student ID, Government-issued ID, or Barangay Clearance at the reception desk and sign the visitor registry logbook.
2. **Acceptable Use**:
   - Use computer terminals for academic research, homework, thesis encoding, online courses, career development, and access to government portals (eGov PH, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG).
3. **Prohibited Activities**:
   - Online video gaming, gambling, viewing explicit content, or downloading unauthorized software.
   - Eating, drinking, or placing open liquid containers near computer terminals and electronic equipment.
4. **Fair Time Allocation**:
   - During peak hours, individual computer sessions may be capped (e.g. 1 to 2 hours) to give all visiting learners and researchers equal opportunity.
