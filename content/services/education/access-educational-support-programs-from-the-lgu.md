# Access Educational Support Programs from the LGU — Morong, Rizal

The Municipal Government of Morong provides educational support services for learners, working students, out-of-school youth, and educators through the **Special Education Fund (SEF)**, the **Public Employment Service Office (PESO)**, the **Morong Youth Development Office (MYDO)**, and the **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)**.

---

## 1) Special Education Fund (SEF) Programs

**What’s Offered:**

- **Classroom Facilities & Repair**: Funding for school building maintenance, electrical upgrades, and disaster-resilient facilities in coordination with the Local School Board (LSB).
- **Public School Supplies & Learning Kits**: Distribution of school materials for kindergarten and elementary pupils across Morong public schools.
- **Support for School Teachers & Competitions**: Municipal subsidy for instructional materials, District Meet athletic delegations, academic quizzes, and cultural festivals.

**Coordinating Office:**

- Local School Board (LSB) & Office of the Municipal Mayor, Municipal Hall Compound.

---

## 2) Special Program for Employment of Students (SPES) via PESO Morong

**What’s Offered:**

- Summer and mid-year short-term employment program for underprivileged but deserving high school, vocational, or college students aged **15 to 30 years old**.
- Provides practical work experience in municipal offices or partner community centers with 60% of wages paid by the Morong LGU and 40% subsidized by the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE).

**Requirements:**

- Accomplished SPES Application Form.
- Copy of Birth Certificate / Valid ID verifying age between 15 and 30 years old.
- Copy of latest grades / school rating with average passing grade.
- Proof of family income (ITR or Barangay Certificate of Indigency showing parents' combined income below poverty threshold).

**How to Apply:**

1. Monitor announcements from the **Public Employment Service Office (PESO) Morong**.
2. Submit your application portfolio at the PESO Morong window, Municipal Hall.
3. Attend pre-employment screening and orientation.

## 3) Morong Youth Development Office (MYDO) Programs

**What’s Offered:**

- Leadership training, youth governance seminars, and endorsement to the **National Youth Commission's Youth Organization Registration Program (YORP)**.
- Alternative Learning System (ALS) community advocacy and assistance for out-of-school youth seeking high school equivalency.
- Contact: `steventantiongcodeleon@gmail.com` | Morong Municipal Hall.

---

## 4) Crisis Educational Financial Aid via MSWDO

**What’s Offered:**

- One-time emergency educational assistance for indigent students under the **Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS)**.
- Intended to cover urgent school project costs, graduation fees, or tuition balances during unexpected family financial emergencies.

**Requirements:**

- Certificate of Enrollment or School Registration Form.
- Barangay Certificate of Indigency.
- Valid ID of parent/guardian and student.
- Case interview and assessment by an MSWDO social worker.

## 5) Morong, Rizal e-Library (Public Digital Learning & Research Hub)

> **Theme:** _"Morong E-Library, Connecting Minds, Empowering Futures"_

- **Overview**: A public digital learning center established by the Municipal Government of Morong, funded through the incentive grant earned from the municipality's conferment of the **Seal of Good Local Governance (SGLG)** by the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG).
- **Available Amenities**: Nine (9) desktop computers (pre-installed with **DOST STARBOOKS** digital science research kiosks), two (2) printers, one (1) photocopy machine, two (2) 45” Smart TVs, one (1) 65” Smart TV, and one (1) 21” Full HD monitor.
- **Operating Schedule**: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM (**100% Free Public Access**).
- **Address**: Gov. Tupas St., Brgy. San Pedro, Morong, Philippines, 1960.
