# Family & Community Welfare Support Services — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** provides counseling, crisis intervention, child and youth welfare protection, and women's empowerment services across the Municipality of Morong.

---

## Welfare Programs & Protection Services

1. **Child Protection & Youth Welfare (CICL & CAR)**:
   - Case management and diversion interventions for Children in Conflict with the Law (CICL) and Children at Risk (CAR) pursuant to Republic Act No. 9344.
   - Child abuse prevention and psychosocial support.
2. **LCAT-VAWC & Women's Protection**:
   - The **Local Committee on Anti-Trafficking and Violence Against Women and Children (LCAT-VAWC)** coordinates rapid crisis intervention and temporary shelter placement for victims of domestic abuse and exploitation under RA 9262.
   - Direct collaboration with the PNP Morong Women and Children Protection Desk.
3. **Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counseling (PMOC)**:
   - Mandatory family planning, marital communication, and responsible parenting seminar for couples applying for a marriage license.
4. **Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS)**:
   - Medical subsidies, hospital guarantee letters, laboratory test aid, and transportation assistance for indigent patients referred to regional hospitals.
5. **Daycare Services (Early Childhood Care and Development)**:
   - Supervision of public Daycare Centers across all 8 Morong barangays providing supplemental feeding, foundational socialization, and early learning.
