# Senior Citizen, Solo Parent & PWD Assistance — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** of Morong administers social protection programs, ID issuances, and statutory financial benefits for Senior Citizens, Solo Parents, and Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) residing across the municipality's 8 barangays.

---

## 1. Senior Citizens (OSCA ID & Benefits)

In compliance with Republic Act No. 9994 (Expanded Senior Citizens Act):

- **Eligibility**: Morong residents aged 60 years and above.
- **Benefits**:
  - 20% discount and VAT exemption on medicines, basic groceries, public transport, medical consultations, and dining.
  - OSCA Purchase Booklet for medicines and prime commodities.
  - Social Pension for indigent senior citizens (PhP 1,000 monthly distributed quarterly).
  - Centenarian cash gift (RA 10868) and provincial milestone incentives.
- **Requirements**:
  - PSA Birth Certificate or valid Government ID showing birthdate.
  - Barangay Certificate of Residency in Morong.
  - Two (2) 1x1 ID pictures.

---

## 2. Solo Parents (RA 11861 Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act)

- **Eligibility**: Any parent bearing sole parental responsibility over child(ren) under 18 years old (or over 18 if disabled/dependent).
- **Benefits**:
  - Comprehensive medical and livelihood program access.
  - Monthly cash subsidy of PhP 1,000 for minimum-wage and low-income solo parents.
  - Flexible work schedule and parental leave entitlements.
  - Educational scholarship priority for dependent children.
- **Requirements**:
  - Barangay Certification of Solo Parent status (attesting living condition for at least 6 months).
  - PSA Birth Certificate(s) of dependent child(ren).
  - Proof of income (ITR, payslip, or Certificate of Indigency).
  - Supporting documents (Death Certificate of spouse, legal separation decree, or abandonment certification).

---

## 3. Persons with Disabilities (PWD ID & Assistance)

In accordance with Republic Act No. 10754:

- **Eligibility**: Any Morong resident with long-term physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory impairments.
- **Benefits**:
  - 20% discount and VAT exemption on medicine, dental/medical services, diagnostic tests, transport, and lodging.
  - Assistive devices (wheelchairs, crutches, walking canes) distributed through MSWDO and provincial health programs.
- **Requirements**:
  - Medical Certificate or Disability Assessment signed by a licensed government or private physician.
  - Barangay Certificate of Residency.
  - Two (2) 1x1 ID pictures.
