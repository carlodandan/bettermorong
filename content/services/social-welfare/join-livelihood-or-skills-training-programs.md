# Livelihood & Skills Training Programs — Morong, Rizal

The **Municipality of Morong**, through the collaborative efforts of the **MSWDO**, the **Public Employment Service Office (PESO)**, and **TESDA**, provides free technical skills training, entrepreneurship seminars, and livelihood starter kits for unemployed adults, women, out-of-school youth, and displaced workers.

---

## Technical & Vocational Courses Offered

1. **Culinary & Native Food Processing**:
   - Production of traditional Morong culinary products, including _Balaw-balaw_ preservation, baking, and commercial meat processing.
2. **Sewing, Dressmaking & Tailoring**:
   - Garment construction, eco-bag production, and school uniform assembly.
3. **Electrical, Automotive & Construction Trades**:
   - Domestic electrical installation and maintenance (NC II).
   - Small engine maintenance and plumbing.
4. **Information Technology & Digital Livelihood**:
   - Basic computer literacy, online business bookkeeping, and virtual administrative support.
5. **Beauty Care & Wellness**:
   - Cosmetology, hairdressing, and therapeutic massage therapy.

---

## Eligibility & Benefits

- Open to all bonafide Morong residents aged 18 years and above.
- **Benefits**:
  - Free tuition and training modules.
  - TESDA National Certificate (NC II) assessment voucher upon course completion.
  - Livelihood seed starter tools and linkage to DTI Negosyo Center programs.

---

## How to Register

1. **Submit Documents to PESO / MSWDO**:
   - Barangay Certificate of Residency and Certificate of Indigency.
   - Valid Government ID or PSA Birth Certificate.
   - Two (2) 2x2 colored ID photos with white background.
2. **Office Location**:
   - **PESO / MSWDO Desk**: Ground Floor, Morong Municipal Hall, Sitio Paglabas, San Pedro, Morong, Rizal.
   - **Office Hours**: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
   - **Course Fee**: **100% Free** (Subsidized by LGU Morong and TESDA).
