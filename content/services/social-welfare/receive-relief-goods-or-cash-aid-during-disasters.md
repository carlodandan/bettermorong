# Disaster Relief Goods & Emergency Cash Aid — Morong, Rizal

During typhoons, heavy monsoon flooding, fires, or natural disasters, the **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)**, in coordination with the **Morong MDRRMO** and DSWD Field Office IV-A, deploys emergency food packs, hygiene kits, and emergency financial assistance to affected families across Morong.

---

## 1. Family Food Packs & Emergency Relief Supplies

### What is Distributed:

- **DSWD / LGU Family Food Packs**: Rice, canned meats, noodles, and drinking water.
- **Hygiene & Sanitation Kits**: Toothbrush, soap, laundry detergent, and sanitary pads.
- **Sleeping & Shelter Kits**: Blankets, sleeping mats, and tarpaulins for displaced families.

### How Distribution Works:

1. **Evacuation Centers**: Displaced residents in designated municipal evacuation centers (school gyms, barangay covered courts, and Morong Municipal Gym) are registered in the Disaster Assistance Family Access Card (DAFAC) log upon entry.
2. **Community Relief Delivery**: For families isolated in flooded low-lying areas or lakeshore communities (Bombongan, CCL, San Juan, Lagundi), relief goods are dispatched via barangay disaster coordinators and MDRRMO rescue boats.

---

## 2. Emergency Cash Assistance & Shelter Grants

### Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS):

- **Medical & Burial Cash Assistance**: Emergency financial grants for families suffering casualties or critical injuries due to natural calamities.
- **Disaster Rehabilitation Shelter Support (DRRAP / NHA Grants)**: Building materials and financial grants for households with partially or totally damaged dwellings following major disasters.

### Requirements to Avail of Cash Aid:

1. Valid Government-issued ID.
2. Barangay Certification of Disaster Damage / DAFAC Card.
3. Photos of damaged residential structure.
4. MSWDO Social Case Study Report.
