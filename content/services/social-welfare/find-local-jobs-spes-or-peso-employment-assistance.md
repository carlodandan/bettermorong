# Find Local Jobs, SPES, or PESO Employment Assistance — Morong, Rizal

The **Public Employment Service Office (PESO)** of Morong, Rizal connects jobseekers with local and regional employers, facilitates student employment through **SPES**, coordinates emergency community wage programs (**TUPAD**), and assists Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs).

## 1. Local Job Matching & Referral

Jobseekers residing in Morong can register in the municipal labor pool to be referred to hiring companies in Rizal and CALABARZON:

### Requirements to Register

1. **Updated Resume / Bio-data** (with recent 2x2 photo).
2. **Barangay Clearance for Employment** (from your Morong barangay).
3. Photocopy of **Transcript of Records (TOR)**, **Diploma**, or **High School Card**.
4. **Valid Government-Issued ID** or Community Tax Certificate (Cedula).
5. For technical/skilled positions: **TESDA National Certificate (NC II/NC III)** or PRC license.

### Procedure

1. Fill out the National Skills Registration System (NSRS) / PESO Jobseeker Registration Form at the PESO desk.
2. Undergo initial profile assessment and skills interview.
3. Receive official **Referral Letter** to matched employer partner companies for interview and placement.

## 2. Special Program for the Employment of Students (SPES)

SPES helps poor but deserving students pursue their education by providing paid employment during school breaks (summer / Christmas vacation):

### Eligibility Criteria

- **Age**: 15 to 30 years old.
- **Residency**: Must be a bona fide resident of Morong, Rizal.
- **Family Income**: Combined annual net income of parents must not exceed the regional poverty threshold.
- **Academic Standing**: Passing grades in the preceding school term (no failing marks).

### Documentary Requirements

1. Duly accomplished **SPES Application Form**.
2. **Copy of Grades**: Form 138 (report card) for high school students, or recent semester registration form with grades for college/vocational students.
3. **Birth Certificate** (PSA or Local Civil Registry copy).
4. **Proof of Family Income**: Latest Income Tax Return (ITR) of parents, BIR Certificate of Tax Exemption, or Barangay Certificate of Indigency.
5. School ID and Barangay Clearance.

## 3. DOLE TUPAD Emergency Wage Employment

The **Tulong Panghanapbuhay sa Ating Disadvantaged/Displaced Workers (TUPAD)** program provides short-term emergency employment (10 to 30 days) for displaced, seasonal, or underemployed workers in Morong:

- **Work Assigned**: Community declogging of canals, road cleaning, tree planting, coastal cleanup along Morong River and Laguna de Bay shores, and school maintenance.
- **Compensation**: Daily minimum wage rate for CALABARZON, with GSIS accident insurance coverage provided by DOLE.
- **How to Apply**: Intake is coordinated periodically through Morong PESO and Barangay Councils. Inquire at the PESO desk or your Barangay Hall.

---

## 4. OFW Help Desk

Returning or distressed Overseas Filipino Workers and their families can access:

- Reintegration livelihood and capital assistance programs through OWWA.
- Pre-departure orientations and anti-illegal recruitment advisory.
- Verification of licensed overseas recruitment agencies.
